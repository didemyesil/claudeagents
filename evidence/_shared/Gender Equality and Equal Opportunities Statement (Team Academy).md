## Statement

Team Academy is committed to equal opportunity and non-discriminatory treatment for all members of its community — learners, faculty, staff, and external partners — regardless of gender, race, ethnicity, age, disability, sexual orientation, religion, nationality, or socio-economic background. This commitment applies across all of Team Academy's programmes, including the Professional Master's in Applied AI, the Bachelor's in Applied Computing, and the Tech MBA.

This statement consolidates, and makes explicit as a single institution-wide concept, the non-discrimination and equal-opportunity commitments that already operate in practice across Team Academy's existing policies:

**In admissions.** Team Academy's Policy for Quality Assurance (§ESG 1.4, Student Admission) already states that "all applicants from diverse backgrounds, cultures, genders and nationalities, regardless of their physical disability, are encouraged to apply to Team Academy programmes," and that all applicants "are dealt with professionally and given information and guidance" on a non-discriminatory basis. This statement affirms that commitment as an institution-wide equal-opportunities principle, not an admissions-specific provision alone.

**In assessment.** The Assessment Policy's Student Protection section commits Team Academy to "fostering a diverse and inclusive learning environment, recognising and respecting intercultural differences that may influence assessment dynamics," and requires assessors to "design and evaluate assessments that are free from cultural assumptions or references unfamiliar to some learners." The same section commits the institution to providing accommodations for learners with disabilities "in line with individual needs," handled confidentially, "promoting equal opportunities for success." The Teaching and Examination Regulations (Article on Inclusive Assessment and Examination Provisions) give this practical effect: learners with permanent or temporary disabilities — including chronic illness, physical disability, sensory impairment, learning difficulties, psychological conditions, or neurodivergence — may request reasonable adjustments (extended time, alternative examination formats, adapted physical environment, assistive technology, alternative assessment forms), decided by the Examination Board without compromising academic standards.

**In external stakeholder governance.** The Responsibilities and Guidelines for the Field Representatives already require that "the selection of further members will also take into consideration status, gender, race, ethnicity, age and geographic locations," so that the external body advising on curriculum and programme quality itself reflects diverse perspectives.

**Institution-wide principle.** Taken together, these provisions reflect a consistent institutional practice: Team Academy does not tolerate intolerance or discrimination in any of its academic or governance activities, and actively designs its admissions, assessment, and advisory structures to promote equal opportunity. This statement makes that practice explicit as a named, standalone institutional concept — answering Sample Question 14 under IAAR Standard 1 directly — while future, more detailed policy elaboration (e.g. a dedicated staff-recruitment non-discrimination clause, a formal grievance procedure specific to discrimination complaints) remains an area Team Academy intends to continue developing as the cluster's programmes launch and operate.

## Scope and status

This statement applies institution-wide, across all three programmes in the current accreditation cluster. It is currently an internal governance statement, consistent with the QA Policy's own current internal-only publication status (see the shared QA Policy, ESG 1.8) — both are planned for public publication once the cluster's programmes are operational.
