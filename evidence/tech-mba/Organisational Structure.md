## Role Descriptions

**The Executive Board** provides overall governance and strategic direction for Team Academy, including the Tech MBA.

**The Examination Board** is responsible for ensuring the quality and integrity of assessment processes for the Tech MBA, overseeing examination standards, and safeguarding the academic quality of the programme and the level of the diploma — see `Examination Board Handbook.md` (this evidence folder).

**QA Team** provides quality assurance oversight for the Tech MBA, ensuring academic standards and quality measures are maintained, per the institution-wide QA Policy.

**The Educational Council (Student Advisory Board)** consists of student representatives, including Tech MBA learners, who share their experience of programme quality and provide advisory input. Their recommendations are passed to the Managing Director, who shares them with the Programme Manager.

**Managing Director** oversees operational management of Team Academy, coordinating between departments and implementing strategic decisions from the Executive Board.

**Programme Manager (Tech MBA)** manages the Tech MBA's day-to-day administration and coordination between faculty, learners, examiners and the curriculum team — the Tech MBA's own Programme Manager role is described in the Tech MBA Programme Handbook.

**Programme Design & Development Lead** is responsible for designing and developing the Tech MBA curriculum, ensuring curriculum quality and improvement.

**Faculty** are the academic staff who design and deliver the Tech MBA's learning activities and module projects, as part of the curriculum team, and evaluate assessments.

**Internal Examiners / External Examiner** — specific to the Tech MBA's Final Project assessment: two Internal Examiners review the Final Project submission; where they do not agree, an External Examiner is appointed to provide the deciding evaluation (see `Examination Board Handbook.md` and `Policy on Assessment.md`, this evidence folder).

**Admissions Manager** handles Tech MBA admissions processes.

**Finance** manages financial operations, budgeting, and financial reporting institution-wide.

**Marketing** is responsible for marketing activities and external communications, including for the Tech MBA.

**Web Development** maintains Team Academy's web presence.
