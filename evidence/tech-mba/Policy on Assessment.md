## Part A: Institution-Wide Assessment Policy (shared with the Master's and Bachelor's programmes)

Team Academy has adopted competency-based learning and assessment for all of its programmes. The vision on assessment — that assessment is "the fulfilment of the promise of competency-based learning and the start of future continual learning and growth of each member of its community" — applies equally to the Tech MBA.

**Regulatory, societal and academic context**, the **assessment cycle** (Design → Development → Administering → Assessment → Analysis → Feedback & Feedforward → Revision), and the **reach** of the Assessment Policy within Team Academy's documentary hierarchy (below the strategic/educational policies, aligned with the Teaching and Examination Regulations) apply to the Tech MBA exactly as they apply to the Master's and Bachelor's programmes — see Appendix 15 (Master's copy) for the full institution-wide text, reproduced here by reference rather than duplicated, since it is unchanged for the Tech MBA.

**Generic standards for the institute** — the design, development, administering, assessment, analysis, feedback/feedforward and revision principles set out in Part A of the Master's Assessment Policy (alignment with learning outcomes, diversity of assessment methods, inclusivity, validity, reliability, transparency in criteria/caesura, objectivity, timeliness, four-eye checking) apply institution-wide, including to the Tech MBA.

## Part B: Programme-Specific Assessment Policy — Tech MBA

This section describes the procedural policies for assessment within the Tech MBA. It follows the institutional Assessment Policy (Part A) and clarifies Tech MBA-specific details.

### Assessment Structure

The Tech MBA's summative assessment system is organised into four parts, corresponding to the programme's four certifications (Transform your Business; Lead Your Business; Innovate and Grow Your Business; Leadership and Action Research), each made up of a variety of interim assessments that are weighted and combine into a final qualification score (out of 100, with a pass caesura of 60).

**Module Projects** — one per module, weighted at 50% of the relevant assessment component, evaluated by faculty members, and central to the learning experience: they allow learners to apply module content to real-world scenarios in a way consistent with the programme's competency-based, entrepreneurial-mindset focus.

**Final Project** — the capstone assessment, comprising: (1) a work-based project plan, (2) a written report, (3) a video presentation, and (4) a live Q&A session. Learners must have achieved at least an average of 60 across the preceding certifications before proceeding to the Final Project module. The Final Project is assessed by two Internal Examiners; where they do not agree on a Pass/Fail decision, an External Examiner is appointed within two weeks to review the project against the assessment criteria and provide the deciding evaluation. If the External Examiner decides on a Fail, the candidate does not graduate; if a Pass, the candidate proceeds to the oral presentation/Q&A stage with the Internal Examiners.

### Roles and Responsibilities

- **Programme Manager** (Tech MBA): manages the day-to-day coordination of assessment, checks Final Project submissions against formatting/completeness requirements, and liaises between learners, examiners and the Examination Board.
- **Faculty / Module Assessors**: evaluate module projects and provide expert feedback.
- **Internal Examiners** (2): review and grade the Final Project's written report and, where relevant, the video presentation.
- **External Examiner**: appointed only where the two Internal Examiners disagree on Pass/Fail, to provide the deciding assessment.
- **Examination Board**: safeguards the quality of testing, examinations and final assessments for the Tech MBA in the same institution-wide role it holds for Team Academy's other programmes (see the Examination Board Handbook — Tech MBA), including handling academic integrity cases and appeals.

### Academic Integrity in Assessment

Consistent with the institution-wide Academic Integrity provisions (Part A, and the Tech MBA Teaching and Examination Regulations, Article 3.6), academic dishonesty in assessed work (fraud, forgery, academic outsourcing, plagiarism) is escalated by the supervisor/assessor to the Programme Manager, who follows the formal institutional procedure — closing the gap previously noted in the Tech MBA Supervision Guide, which mentioned academic integrity only once, in passing, without a codified assessment-level procedure behind it.

### Inclusive Assessment

Consistent with Part A and with the Tech MBA Teaching and Examination Regulations (Article 3.5), learners with disabilities, chronic illness, neurodiversity or other special personal circumstances may request reasonable adjustments to standard assessment conditions, decided by the Examination Board.
