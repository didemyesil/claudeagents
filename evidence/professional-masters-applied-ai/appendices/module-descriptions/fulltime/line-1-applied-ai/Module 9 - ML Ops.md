# Module 9: ML Ops (Full Time)

**Code:** 09.AI | **Learning Line:** Applied Artificial Intelligence | **ECs:** 6

Introduces the principles and practices of ML Ops for applied AI: implementing models in cloud environments, managing deployment pipelines for scalable business applications, monitoring models in production (A/B testing), detecting data drift, and retraining strategies for performance degradation while maintaining efficiency at scale. A project consolidates these skills through design, deployment, and monitoring of a production-level ML system.

## Connection with Applied Research

ML Ops bridges building AI models and sustaining their value in real-world use. The Applied Research line adds a layer questioning how deployment/monitoring/retraining practices affect organisational processes, stakeholder trust, and long-term outcomes — ML Ops is about technical efficiency and about maintaining relevant, reliable, responsible AI systems.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**ML Ops** — Deploy models in cloud environments to support scalable business applications; monitor model performance using A/B testing to inform deployment decisions; detect data drift in production environments and evaluate its impact on model reliability; retrain machine learning models in response to performance degradation while ensuring scalability and efficiency.
