# Module 2: Self-Inquiry in Professional Practice

**Year:** 1 | **Code:** 02.RP | **Learning Line:** Research Practice Line | **ECs:** 7.5

Shared across all three tracks (SE, DS, ML). Introduces self-inquiry as the first stage of the Research Practice Line: the Plan–Act–Observe–Reflect cycle applied to one's own actions/habits/professional behaviour. Learners formulate a self-inquiry question, plan/test a small action, collect observations/feedback, reflect, and apply a justified improvement.

## Connection with the Professional Practice Line

Self-inquiry practised through authentic situations from technical learning, project work, collaboration, time management, feedback, professional behaviour — grounded in real practice, not an abstract exercise.

## Intended Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Self-inquiry** — formulate simple self-inquiry questions to improve professional practice; apply self-improvement opportunities in one's own practice.

## Module Planning (5 weeks + seasonal application)

Week 1: Action Inquiry Orientation. Week 2: Inquiry Focus and Question. Week 3: Plan and Micro-experiment. Week 4: Act, Observe and Reflect. Week 5: Apply Improvement and Prepare for Certification VIVA.

**Seasonal Application:** ~3 further sprints as self-inquiry cycles during the season (Plan/Act/Observe/Reflect via squad work, standups, retrospectives, team coaching).

**Vertical Leadership Integration:** vertical leadership assessment at year start; conversations tracked via Proairesis Platform; programme goal is development toward "Expert" by graduation. Season 1 focus: self-awareness, accountability, openness to feedback, team contribution, disciplined agile participation.

## Weekly Study Load (41 hrs total)

Self-inquiry practice/Module Project (blended): 25 | Self-study (online): 4 | Workshops (online): 2 | Standups (campus): 2 | Squads (campus): 4 | Team Coaching (campus): 4

## Assessment

**Low-stake:** workshop/sprint reflections, standup participation, squad participation, team coaching contributions.
**Medium-stake:** Module Project (one complete self-inquiry cycle — question, action plan, observation logs, reflection, feedback, applied improvement, synthesis).
**High-stake:** Certification VIVA (end of Certification 1).

**Passing criteria:** ≥75% low-stake, ≥75% medium-stake, Certification VIVA passed.
