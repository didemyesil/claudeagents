# Faculty Roles in the Professional Master's in Applied AI

This document translates our values and pedagogical philosophy into profiles for the key roles held by faculty and staff at Team Academy's *Professional Master's in Applied Artificial Intelligence*. These profiles describe the roles for professionals who facilitate learning and support the development of our learners during their academic journey.

As a professional, practice-based programme aligned with the Dutch standards for a Professional Master's (NLQF), we work with a unique learning model and community. Faculty members are not traditional lecturers but **facilitators**, **coaches** and **supervisors, assessors** who guide and challenge learners as they develop both technical mastery, leadership and action research capacity.

This document sets out clear roles, core competencies, and minimum criteria for these roles to uphold quality standards across the programme.

## 1. The Facilitator

### Role

Facilitators at Team Academy lead workshops across the Professional Master's in Applied AI. Their primary responsibility is to activate and support participatory learning aligned with the programme's competency-based model. Facilitators design and deliver interactive sessions that build on the digital content and help learners towards their assignments, using practical approaches such as hands-on coding, discussions, peer exchange, and inquiry-based learning, depending on the subject matter.

In the learning community of Team Academy, facilitators act not as lecturers but as co-creators of learning environments. They integrate their strong industry expertise into engaging sessions where learners connect theory to practice and reflect on their learning. Their role is to foster a dynamic community of inquiry, peer learning, and experimentation.

Facilitators carry out the following responsibilities:

- Prepare and deliver workshops in alignment with programme competencies and learning outcomes, as well as the learners' needs
- Guide learners in applying theoretical knowledge to practical challenges
- Foster a collaborative learning culture that values curiosity, openness, and shared responsibility
- Facilitate critical reflection, dialogue, and group problem-solving
- Contribute to the continuous development of modules by providing feedback to programme teams
- Collaborate with coaches and supervisors to ensure alignment across the learning journey
- Maintain high standards of accessibility, inclusion, and academic integrity
- Support formative assessment and offer developmental feedback during sessions
- Encourage learner autonomy while providing structure and clarity

## 3. Team Coach

### Role

Team Coaches at Team Academy support the learning community by facilitating group dialogue, reflective practice, and interpersonal development across the Professional Master's in Applied AI. Their role is to create a safe, challenging, and collaborative environment where learners can deepen their leadership awareness, enhance interpersonal effectiveness, and strengthen their capacity to work in complex professional settings.

Team Coaches do not function as traditional academic instructors. Instead, they act as process facilitators who guide cohorts in developing shared responsibility, self-directed learning habits, and the relational competencies that especially underpin the programme's leadership and action-research learning lines. They help learners reflect on their experiences, navigate group dynamics, and stay connected to their personal and collective learning intentions.

Team Coaches carry out the following responsibilities:

- Facilitate regular group sessions that nurture reflective practice, peer learning, and collaborative sense-making.
- Support learners in developing leadership competencies such as self-awareness, interpersonal awareness, and responsible participation (aligned with the Applied Research Learning Line).
- Hold space for constructive dialogue on learning challenges, professional identity, and group dynamics in a psychologically safe manner.
- Guide learners in setting personal and collective learning intentions, reviewing progress, and adjusting actions throughout the module or programme phase.
- Observe and address emerging group dynamics and support the cohort in building healthy communication, trust, and accountability.
- Connect group discussions with programme competencies and learning outcomes to reinforce coherence across the learning journey.
- Collaborate with other faculty and programme managers to ensure alignment in learner support, flagging any concerns regarding engagement, participation, or well-being.
- Provide developmental, process-focused feedback that strengthens learners' autonomy, presence, and leadership practice.
- Participate in internal calibration, reflection, and development activities with programme teams to maintain high standards of coaching practice.
- Maintain professional boundaries, ensuring ethical conduct, confidentiality, and alignment with the organisation's leadership philosophy and values.

## 4. Assessor

### Role

Assessors at Team Academy are appointed by the Examination Board and are responsible for designing, developing, administering, and grading assessments across modules in the Professional Master's. They ensure alignment with the university's Assessment Policy and programme learning outcomes. Assessors contribute to a fair and transparent assessment system, upholding academic standards and ensuring assessment quality and consistency throughout the programme.

Assessors carry out the following responsibilities:

- Design valid, reliable, and transparent assessments aligned with the university assessment policy and learning outcomes
- Develop assignments, projects, presentations, and evaluation rubrics using constructive alignment
- Administer assessments in accordance with the university's ethical standards and academic integrity policies
- Evaluate student submissions fairly using predefined rubrics, and apply the university's standard procedures for evaluation
- Provide clear, actionable feedback and feedforward to support student learning
- Analyse and refine assessments and tools regularly based on data and stakeholder input
- Comply with the 4-Eye Policy, assessment regulations, and guidelines from the Examination Board
- Communicate assessment protocols and expectations clearly to students
- Participate in institutional assessment-related quality assurance processes
- Uphold assessment ethics, including objectivity, fairness, and impartiality
- Possess sufficient subject matter and linguistic proficiency to assess effectively and communicate with learners

## 5. Supervisor

### Role

Supervisors at Team Academy guide learners through the Final Project's Assessment, supporting them in designing and executing a project that integrates technical skills, leadership, and action research in alignment with programme outcomes and NLQF Level 7 professional standards. Their role is to facilitate academic and professional reflection, provide structured feedback, and ensure rigour, relevance, and integrity throughout the assessment process. Supervisors do not offer solutions but enable learners to make well-reasoned, ethical, and autonomous decisions, collaborating closely with programme management to uphold quality and coherence.

Supervisors carry out the following responsibilities:

- Support learners in scoping and defining their final project topic and approach, to ensure alignment with programme competencies and assessment criteria
- Provide timely and constructive feedback on drafts and promoting clarity, coherence, and critical thinking
- Guide learners in demonstrating academic integrity, ethical awareness, and responsible leadership
- Monitor learner progress through regular supervision check-ins, as outlined in the supervision guidelines
- Liaise with the programme manager to flag concerns and support learner development
- Contribute to internal quality assurance by reflecting on supervision practices and providing input on the final assessment process

## Competency Profile for Faculty Members

- **Interpersonal Skills** — Demonstrate care, courage, and collaboration in all interpersonal interactions. Communicate openly and respectfully with learners, colleagues, and stakeholders. Build trust through active listening, presence, and integrity. Create spaces where all voices are invited and acknowledged.
- **Commitment to Growth** — Model accountability and courage in personal and professional development. Stay responsive to rapid shifts in your domain. Continuously reflect on and evolve your practice to meet the needs of a changing world and an evolving learning community.
- **Curriculum Development** — Actively contribute to designing engaging, learner-centred curricula. Act as an active member of the curriculum team who always strives to improve programmes that are up-to-date with academic and industry standards.
- **Facilitative Teaching & Supervision** — Use active, participatory methods that nurture critical thinking, real-world problem solving, and ethical leadership. Guide learners through ambiguity and complexity while supporting their autonomy and voice.
- **Competency-Based Assessment & Feedback** — Implement assessments that are transparent, purposeful, and developmental. Offer feedback that promotes self-awareness, learning ownership, and leadership growth. Ensure assessments reflect not only academic mastery but also reflective practice and ethical reasoning.
- **Contribution to the Learning Culture** — Actively shape and uphold the culture of shared responsibility and participation at Team Academy. Bring your voice to organisational development. Engage in honest reflection and dialogue to improve systems, policies, and community practices.
- **Mentorship & Leadership Practice** — Model responsible and relational leadership. Coach other colleagues with humility and respect. Share your experiences and learnings generously. Hold space for others' growth and actively contribute to a leadership culture rooted in trust, learning, and collective impact.

## Selection & Hiring Criteria

To be eligible for the role of faculty at Team Academy, the following criteria apply:

- Holds at least a Master's degree, preferably a PhD, in a relevant field (e.g. Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Organisational Leadership, Action Research)
- Demonstrates relevant industry experience, enabling practical translation of concepts
- Has experience in teaching and assessment in higher education
- Possesses a strong command of English (C1 level or above)
- Shows commitment to ongoing professional development and learning agility
- Understands and aligns with the Team Academy leadership manifesto and learning philosophy
- Demonstrates cultural competence and ability to foster inclusive learning environments
