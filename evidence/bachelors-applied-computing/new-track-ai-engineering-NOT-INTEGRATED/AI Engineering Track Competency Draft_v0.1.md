## Purpose

First draft of the track competencies for a proposed fourth track, Applied AI Engineering, within the Bachelor of Science in Applied Computing.

## Proposed Track Competencies

| Programme Outcome | Applied AI Engineering Competency Set |
|---|---|
| **Analyse** | AI solution requirements analysis |
| **Advise** | Applied Research for Professional Practice; Professional advice |
| **Design** | AI system architecture design; Context and retrieval pipeline design; Evaluation and guardrail design |
| **Build** | Programming; Data structures and algorithms; Database development; Foundation model application development; Agentic workflow development; Evaluation and error analysis; Agent-assisted software development |
| **Manage & Control** | Software development process management; AI system operations and monitoring; Responsible AI and compliance management |
| **Communicate & Collaborate** | Self-inquiry; One-to-one inquiry; Team inquiry |
