# Bachelor of Science in Applied Computing — Programme Brochure

**Develop the skills to build digital solutions in a rapidly evolving world**

## The Programme at a Glance

- **Programme Title:** Bachelor of Science in Applied Computing
- **Programme Type:** Higher Professional Education (HBO)
- **Tracks:** Applied Software Engineering, Applied Data Science, Applied Machine Learning
- **Duration:** 4 years (240 ECTS) part-time / 3 years (180 ECTS) full-time & fast track
- **Workload:** ~40 hours/week

## Why Applied Computing?

Digital technology is transforming every sector; organisations need professionals who can build digital solutions, work well with others, communicate clearly, and lead responsibly in fast-changing environments, especially as AI becomes more capable. The programme combines: strong technical skills (software, data, ML depending on track); human skills (communication, collaboration, leadership); agile ways of working (projects, sprint cycles, team-based learning); industry-connected learning (real-life-like projects, hackathons, employer meet-ups); a flexible study format (campus-based with options supporting working professionals). Technical and professional development are integrated throughout, not learned separately.

## Programme Structure

Three specialised tracks: Applied Software Engineering, Applied Data Science, Applied Machine Learning. Each track integrates technical development, project work and professional learning in a practice-based environment mirroring professional practice; students progress from structured/guided learning to increasingly independent professional performance. Available as a 4-year route and a 3-year fast-track route.

## Programme Outcomes

Six core professional outcomes:

| Outcome | Description |

## Programme Overviews

**4-Year Programme:** Year 1 Foundations (fundamental technical skills + intro to applied research); Year 2 Integration (technical competencies via projects/collaboration); Year 3 Specialisation (deepening track expertise, complex challenges); Year 4 Graduation Project (independent project demonstrating all programme outcomes).

**3-Year Fast Track:** Year 1 Foundations (accelerated progression prep); Year 2 Integration (increasing independence/complexity); Year 3 Specialisation & Graduation Project (project starts early, runs parallel with modules).

## Track Overview (3-year Fast Track)

Same Years 1–2 as above. Year 3 (SE): Module 6 Team Inquiry; Module 7 Software Development Process and Quality Management & SE Solution Design using Action Inquiry; Module 8 Advanced SE Practices & SE Graduation Project. (DS/ML tracks follow the analogous pattern with DS/AI-ML Solution Design and Graduation Project combined into Year 3's Modules 7–8.)

## Graduation Project

Final year: individual project in a professional context — analyse a business problem, design and implement a solution, demonstrate competence at professional bachelor's level.

**Which track is for you?** SE — designing/building/improving software systems and digital products. DS — analysing data, generating insight, supporting decision-making. ML — developing intelligent models and solutions that learn from data.

## Prepare for the Real World

- **Traineeship** — integrated in Year 3 (~125 hours): apply technical knowledge in business contexts, collaborate with stakeholders/multidisciplinary teams, gain insight into organisational/industry practices, develop professional/collaborative competencies. Embedded in Year 3 team-based/advanced modules, supports transition to graduation project; students may continue in the same organisational context for their final-year project.
- **Hackathons** — real challenges in a focused, collaborative setting; apply technical knowledge under time pressure, strengthen teamwork/communication/problem-solving.
- **Employer Meet-ups** — meet professionals, gain insight into industry practice/employer expectations, explore career paths, build employer connections.
- **Career Coaching** — understand strengths, shape professional profile, prepare for traineeships/projects/career opportunities.

## The Learning Experience

Workshops (conceptual/technical foundation); Squads (small teams collaborating on projects, reflecting professional practice); Stand-ups (iterative cycles with continuous feedback); Team Coaching (collaboration, reflection, professional development); Projects (increasing complexity requiring competency integration).

## Admissions

**4-Year Programme:** HAVO, VWO, or MBO Level 4 diploma; English B2 or equivalent. Admission: application review + intake interview.

**3-Year Fast Track:** typically VWO diploma or equivalent pre-university qualification; English B2 or equivalent. Admission: application review + intake interview (selective).

**Preparatory Programme:** all students take part upon enrolling; exemption may be requested via intake written/oral assessment.

## Career Opportunities

- Applied Software Engineering — Software Engineer, Digital Product Developer.
- Applied Data Science — Data Scientist, Data Analyst.
- Applied Machine Learning — Machine Learning Engineer, AI Application Developer.
- Graduates may also continue to master's-level education.
