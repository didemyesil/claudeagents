# Team Academy Examination Board Handbook

## Internal Rules, Regulations and Procedures — Examination Board — Tech MBA

*(Adapted from Team Academy's Master's-level Examination Board Handbook — `/evidence/professional-masters-applied-ai/appendices/Appendix 12 - Examination Board Handbook.md` — per Didem's confirmation that Tech MBA shares the Master's regulatory framework. Part 1 below (the Examination Board's own internal rules and regulations) is institution-wide and governs every Examination Board at Team Academy, including the Tech MBA's; it is reproduced here by reference rather than duplicated verbatim, since its content is unchanged for the Tech MBA. ## Part 1: Internal Rules and Regulations for the Functioning of the Examination Board (institution-wide — applies to the Tech MBA's Examination Board without modification)

As set out in the Master's Examination Board Handbook (Appendix 12, Part 1), the following institution-wide provisions govern the Tech MBA's Examination Board exactly as they govern every other Team Academy programme's Examination Board:

- **Installation and independence** (1B): the Examination Board is installed by the Executive Board under the Dutch Higher Education and Scientific Research Act (WHW, Section 7.12a), operates independently of the Executive Board in its assessment decisions, and must include at least one external member; managers with financial responsibility cannot sit on it.
- **Composition** (1C): Team Academy operates a separate Examination Board per programme (or, where appropriate, one Examination Board covering more than one programme with programme-specific TER sections); members are preferably evenly distributed in age, gender and cultural background; at least one internal member must be active faculty in the programme; at least one external member is mandatory; specific exclusions apply (Foundation Board, Executive Board, Directors, Programme Directors, Chairs of Advisory Boards, Student Advisors).
- **Tasks and profiles** (1D): the Chair, Vice-Chair/other members, and Secretary carry the same statutory tasks and job profiles described in the Master's Handbook — independence and expertise in the Chair; subject-matter and assessment expertise in members; administrative/procedural support from the Secretary.
- **Activities of the Examination Board** (1E): the ten statutory tasks under WHW Section 7.12b — determining whether a learner meets the TER's requirements; assuring exam/final-examination quality; setting guidelines within the TER; granting exemptions; taking measures in the event of fraud; assigning assessors; awarding certificates/diplomas; permitting flexible study programmes; issuing transcripts; and producing an annual report — apply to the Tech MBA's Examination Board in full.
- **Decisions of the Examination Board** (1F) and **Complaints Handling** (1G): the same decision-making rules (majority vote, Chair's tie-breaking vote, documented "standard decisions") and complaints procedure (right to a hearing, right to challenge a member's impartiality, right of appeal via the independent NRTO disputes and appeals procedure) apply without modification.

## Part 2: Alignment of the Examination Board's Procedures with the Tech MBA Teaching and Examination Regulations

### General Provisions

The terms defined in the Tech MBA Teaching and Examination Regulations (`Teaching and Examination Regulations (TER).md`, this evidence folder) are the basis for this Part 2. The rules and regulations set out in that TER are reviewed and approved by the Executive Board.

### Programme Structure (per the Tech MBA TER)

The Tech MBA is a fully online programme of approximately 20 months, 60 ECTS (1680 hours), delivered through four certifications (Transform your Business; Lead Your Business; Innovate and Grow Your Business; Leadership and Action Research) plus a Final Project. The Examination Board checks, in line with its institution-wide annual-validation task (Article 2.2 of the Master's TER, applied equivalently here), whether the alignment between programme objectives, outcomes, competencies and assessments continues to meet the required standards, via sampling of module projects and/or Final Project reports.

### Assessment of Learning

The Examination Board safeguards the quality of the Tech MBA's summative assessment system (module projects and the Final Project's four-part submission), consistent with Section 3 of the Tech MBA TER and Part B of the Tech MBA Assessment Policy. For the Final Project specifically, the Examination Board's quality-assurance function is exercised through the Internal Examiner / External Examiner review mechanism already in place per the Tech MBA Supervision Guide, which this Handbook formally recognises as satisfying the Examination Board's statutory quality-assurance role for that assessment.

### Academic Integrity

Cases of suspected plagiarism, fraud, academic outsourcing or other misconduct in Tech MBA assessed work are escalated to the Programme Manager and, for a final decision, to the Examination Board, following the same institution-wide Academic Integrity procedure set out in the Tech MBA TER (Article 3.6) and the Master's Examination Board Handbook (Article 3.13 in that document). This closes the specific gap the evidence map identified for Standard 1: prior to this adaptation, Tech MBA had no Examination Board process behind its one passing mention of "academic integrity" in the Supervision Guide.

### Inclusive Assessment and Examination Provisions

Learners requesting reasonable adjustments (extended time, alternative examination formats, assistive technology) submit their request per the Tech MBA TER (Article 3.5); the Examination Board decides within the institution-wide timeframe, applying the same non-discrimination and equal-opportunity standard used across Team Academy's other programmes.

### Tasks and Responsibilities of the Examination Board (Tech MBA)

As set out in Part 1 above and Section 6 of the Tech MBA TER: determining whether a learner meets the TER's requirements; safeguarding testing/examination/final-assessment quality; establishing guidelines within the TER; monitoring compliance; granting exemptions; deciding on deviation requests; and handling fraud/plagiarism/misconduct cases.

### Hardship Clause

As per Section 7 of the Tech MBA TER, the Executive Board or the Examination Board may allow deviation from the TER's provisions where demonstrable unreasonableness or unfairness of a predominant nature occurs.
