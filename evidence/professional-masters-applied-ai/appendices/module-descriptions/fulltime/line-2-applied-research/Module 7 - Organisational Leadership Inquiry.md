# Module 7: Organisational Leadership Inquiry (Full Time)

**Code:** 07.AR | **Learning Line:** Applied Research | **ECs:** 7

Opens with Action Research IV — Designing and Evaluating Organisational Action Research Interventions: translating organisational challenges into inquiry cycles, designing multi-stakeholder interventions, evaluating outcomes for quality/rigour/ethical responsibility, integrating self- and interpersonal inquiry into systemic perspectives. Develops organisational leadership capacity through Creating Organisational Impact through Interventions, Conversing as Organising, and the EngageForward Process (Framing and Engaging; then Reflecting, Mobilising, and Sustaining). Continues PAC support via group supervision session: The Importance of YOU.

## Connection with Applied AI

Designing/evaluating organisational action research interventions deepens understanding of how AI-driven solutions are introduced into systems shaped by conversational processes; strengthens recognition that organisational impact emerges through interaction, not purely technical decisions.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Organisational Leadership** — Undertake an organisational intervention to address a solution in a professional context; evaluate the outcomes of the intervention using an action research approach with attention to wider organisational impact.

**Research Methodology for Professional Practice** — Apply action research methodology with qualitative/participatory inquiry approaches to design interventions in professional contexts; transfer knowledge generated through action research to one's own professional practice and wider contexts (linked with PAC supervision).
