# Module 4: One-to-one Inquiry in Professional Practice

**Year:** 2 | **Code:** 04.RP | **Learning Line:** Research Practice Line | **ECs:** 7.5

Shared across all three tracks. Second stage of the Research Practice Line, building on Year 1 self-inquiry, shifting focus to learning through purposeful dialogue with others. Applies Plan–Act–Observe–Reflect to a one-to-one interaction: identify a relevant interaction, clarify shared understanding sought, prepare/conduct purposeful dialogue, listen/question, collect feedback, reflect and adapt communication.

## Connection with the Professional Practice Line

Practised through authentic interactions from technical learning, project work, collaboration, feedback, stakeholder engagement — grounded in real interactions, not abstract exercises. Foundation for team inquiry, stakeholder engagement, applied research, and the final project.

## Intended Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**One-to-one inquiry** — build shared understanding in one-to-one interactions with different stakeholders; use feedback to adapt communication in a professional context.

## Module Planning (5 weeks + seasonal application)

Week 1: From Self-inquiry to One-to-one Inquiry. Week 2: Active Listening and Perspective-taking (Listen–Paraphrase–Check). Week 3: Feedback in One-to-one Dialogue. Week 4: Feedback and Communication Adaptation (Situation–Behaviour–Impact). Week 5: Integrated One-to-one Inquiry Cycle and Module Project.

**Seasonal Application:** continues through Season 2's agile rhythm with peers/coaches/technical collaborators/clients/stakeholders. **Vertical Leadership Integration** (Season 2 focus): perspective-taking, active listening, openness to feedback, adaptive communication, relational accountability.

## Weekly Study Load (41 hrs total)

One-to-one inquiry practice/Module Project (blended): 25 | Self-study (online): 4 | Workshops (online): 2 | Standups (campus): 2 | Squads (campus): 4 | Team Coaching (campus): 4

## Assessment

**Low-stake:** workshop/sprint reflections, standup participation, squad participation, team coaching contributions.
**Medium-stake:** Module Project (one complete one-to-one inquiry cycle — interaction context, preparation, dialogue evidence, observations, stakeholder feedback, reflection, communication adaptations, synthesis).
**High-stake:** Certification VIVA (end of Certification 2).

**Passing criteria:** ≥75% low-stake, ≥75% medium-stake, Certification VIVA passed.
