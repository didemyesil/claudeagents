# Module 2: Self-Leadership Inquiry (Full Time)

**Code:** 02.AR | **Learning Line:** Applied Research | **ECs:** 6

Begins with an introductory Action Research session, framing practice-based inquiry questions and positioning challenges within broader conceptual/ethical contexts, recognising how assumptions/biases/values shape interpretation of professional situations and data. Moves into first-person inquiry: exploring values, purpose, aspirations, and ethics; peer inquiry using the Vertical Leadership Inventory (VLI) to investigate recurring leadership patterns; designing a self-directed learning path; concluding with reframing self-leadership, integrating inquiry insights with transformational theory.

## Connection with Applied AI

First-person inquiry into values, assumptions, and leadership stance strengthens critical examination of how technical problems are defined and decisions made — essential for ethical/responsible AI in framing business problems, selecting/preparing data, or designing interventions.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Self-Leadership** — Use inquiry tools to critically reflect on how personal values, beliefs, and habits impact professional practice; develop a personal leadership perspective using insights from action research, peer dialogue, and relevant leadership theories.

**Research Methodology for Professional Practice** — Apply action research methodology with qualitative/participatory inquiry approaches to design interventions in professional contexts.

## Learning Resources

Coghlan (2007) 'Insider action research: Opportunities and challenges', *Action Research*, 5(1); Torbert & Associates (2004) *Action Inquiry: The Secret of Timely and Transforming Leadership*; Rooke & Torbert (2005) 'Seven transformations of leadership', HBR 83(4); Petrie (2014) *Vertical Leadership Development — Part 1* (Center for Creative Leadership); Marshall (2001) 'Self-reflective inquiry practices' in *Handbook of Action Research* (Reason & Bradbury, eds., SAGE).
