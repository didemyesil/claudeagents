# Programme Outcomes of the Tech MBA and the Alignment with Qualification Frameworks

The programme outcomes of the Tech MBA at Schoolvision are based on the needs of organisations that must integrate artificial intelligence and other emerging technologies into their strategy, lead change in an agile way, and innovate towards sustainable growth. In our view, a Tech MBA graduate must be able to work autonomously and responsibly on complex organisational challenges at the intersection of technology, leadership and innovation, and to bring about meaningful, ethical change in their own professional practice. The programme therefore aims to:

- equip learners with the knowledge and skills to strategically integrate AI technologies into business strategies;
- develop agile leaders who can harness the power of emerging technologies to adapt to and lead change in organisations;
- foster an entrepreneurial mindset by exploring sustainable business models and growth strategies to innovate in organisations.

The curriculum integrates three certificates (Transform Your Business, Lead Your Business, and Innovate and Grow Your Business) with a leadership and action research line at the self, interpersonal and organisational levels, and culminates in a work-based final project in which learners improve their own professional practice through action research. Through this integration, learners not only gain strategic command of technology and AI in business, but also learn to lead change collaboratively, ethically and with impact. This approach aligns with level 7 of the Dutch Qualifications Framework (NLQF), the second cycle of the Framework for Qualifications of the European Higher Education Area (QF-EHEA) and the Dublin Descriptors. Thus, the programme outcomes are identified as follows:

**1. Professional Development and Ethical Conduct**
Graduates demonstrate a high level of professional development and learning ability, with a specific focus on strategically integrating AI technologies into business strategies. Ethical and moral conduct is inherent in their work, ensuring that their contributions meet the highest ethical standards while harnessing the power of emerging technologies for organisational change.

**2. Problem-Solving and Action Research**
Graduates independently recognise, analyse and solve complex problems in technology, leadership and innovation, particularly in the context of agile leadership and technology integration. They apply participative research methods and leadership skills effectively while taking ethical, moral and legal considerations into account, with an emphasis on driving organisational change.

**3. Multidisciplinary Collaboration**
Graduates collaborate effectively with professionals from diverse disciplines and perspectives on the integration of AI technologies into business strategies and sustainable growth models. They communicate their technological interpretations, practical research set-up, implications and results professionally, in written, visual and spoken form, fostering an entrepreneurial mindset and driving innovation.

**4. Innovative Impact**
Graduates innovate existing knowledge and practices at the intersection of technology and leadership, aligning these innovations with strategic AI integration, agile leadership and sustainable business models. Their contributions result in a tangible impact on their professional practice and the broader professional domain, with an emphasis on the innovative use of AI and technology for growth and change in organisations.

## The four purposes of higher education

The Council of Europe describes four purposes of higher education. The programme outcomes serve them as follows.

| Purpose of higher education | Programme outcomes that serve it |
|---|---|
| Preparation for sustainable employment | Innovative Impact: graduates create tangible impact through the innovative use of AI and technology for growth and change in organisations. Problem-Solving and Action Research: graduates solve complex problems in technology, leadership and innovation in their own professional context. |
| Preparation for life as active citizens in democratic societies | Professional Development and Ethical Conduct: graduates act ethically and morally and harness emerging technologies responsibly. Problem-Solving and Action Research: graduates take ethical, moral and legal considerations into account. Multidisciplinary Collaboration: graduates work with professionals from diverse disciplines and perspectives. |
| Personal development | Professional Development and Ethical Conduct: graduates demonstrate a high level of professional development and learning ability and reflect on their own leadership. Multidisciplinary Collaboration: graduates communicate professionally in written, visual and spoken form. |
| Development and maintenance of a broad, advanced knowledge base | Problem-Solving and Action Research: graduates apply participative research methods to complex problems. Innovative Impact: graduates innovate existing knowledge and practices at the intersection of technology and leadership, drawing on AI strategy, agile leadership and sustainable business models. |
