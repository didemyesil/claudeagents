# Tech MBA Curriculum (TA Accreditation version, 2023–2025 cohort)

*Note: this reflects an earlier cohort's schedule (starting November 2023). See "Tech MBA Curriculum v10 (CURRENT).md" in this same folder for the institution's current curriculum.*

## TRANSFORM — Understand the Digital Landscape and Transformation

**Module 0 — Onboarding** (Nov 20, 2023)

**Module 1 — Digital Transformation and Business Strategy:** Introduction to Digital Transformation and its Drivers (Wk1); Technology as an Agitator and Enabler (Wk2); Building Blocks of Digital Organizations (Wk3); Competitive Positioning and Future-Proof Business Strategies (Wk4). *2-week Christmas break.*

**Module 2 — Data-Driven Organisations:** Introduction to Data and its Significance for your Organisation (Wk5); Data Management and Governance (Wk6); Data Strategy for Business Growth (Wk7); Data Literacy and Cultivating a Data-Driven Culture (Wk8).

**Module 3 — Deep Dive into AI: From Fundamentals to Business Applications:** Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (Wk9); Machine Learning and Deep Learning (Wk10); Machine and Deep Learning: Business Applications (Wk11); AI Strategy, Capabilities and Center of Excellence (Wk12).

**Module 4 — The Generative Journey: from "Attention is all you need" to Business Revolution:** Introduction to Generative AI (Wk13); Prompt Engineering (Wk14); Business Opportunities and Applications of Generative AI (Wk15); Generative AI Strategy for Business: Taker-Shaper-Maker (Wk16).

**Module 5 — Building Responsible & Ethical AI:** Ethical Implications of AI (Wk17); AI, Sustainability and Social Impact (Wk18); AI Governance and Regulations (Wk19); Singularity and Preparing for the Future (Wk20).

**Module 6 — Elective (Self-paced):** Choose from RPA, VR/AR, Blockchain, IoT (Wks21–24). Break, then VIVAs + Certification (May 27, 2024).

## LEAD — Lead Your Business in an Agile Way

**Module 7 — Understanding and Dealing with Change:** Understand the Concept of Change Management (Wk25); Understand Change Planning Process (Wk26); Common Challenges of Change Management (Wk27); Enhancing Organizational Resilience and Future Trends in Change Management (Wk28).

**Module 8 — Agile Technology Leadership:** Servant Leadership (Wk29); Agile Leadership I — Agile Leader (Wk30); Agile Leadership II — Agile Teams & Organisations (Wk31); Agile Leadership III — Agile Teams & Organisations (Wk32).

**Module 9 — Aligning Agile Teams and Organisations through OKRs:** Introduction to OKRs (Wk33); Using OKRs within an Organisation (Wk34); CFRs and the OKR Life Cycle (Wk35); Improving your Goal-Setting Muscle (Wk36).

**Module 10 — Agile Project Management:** Introduction to Agile Project Management (Wk37); Designing Agile Projects (Wk38); Implementing Agile Methodologies (Wk39); Executing Projects through Sprints (Wk40). Break, then VIVAs + Certification (Sept 30, 2024).

## INNOVATE & GROW — Innovate and Grow Your Business

**Module 11 — Human-Centered Innovation in Business:** Introduction to Design Thinking (Wk41); Empathizing and Defining User Needs (Wk42); Ideation, Prototyping, and Initial Testing (Wk43); Implementation (Wk44).

**Module 12 — Innovative and Sustainable Business Models:** Introduction to New Business Models and Sustainability in the Tech Industry (Wk45); Entrepreneurship and Intrapreneurship (Wk46); Designing Sustainable Business Models with the Business Model Canvas (Wk47); Sustainability Strategies and Implementation (Wk48).

**Module 13 — Growth Hacking:** Introduction to Growth Hacking Fundamentals (Wk49); Exploring New Channels and Platforms for Growth (Wk50); Customer Acquisition and Conversion Optimization (Wk51); Leveraging Data and Automation for Growth (Wk52). *2-week Christmas break*, then Break, then VIVAs + Certification (Jan 13, 2025).

## Leadership and Action Research (LAR)

**Module 1 — Action Research: Exploration Phase** (Wks53–56, Feb 3 – Feb 24, 2025).

**Module 2 — Action Research: Shaping the Project** (Wks57–60, Mar 3 – Mar 24, 2025).

**Module 3 — Action Research: Further Inquiry** (Wks61–64, Mar 31 – Apr 21, 2025).

**Module 4 — Action Research: Bringing All Together** (Wks65–68, Apr 28 – May 19, 2025).

## Final Project Report Writing

**Final Project (supervision)** — weekly supervision sessions Wks69–87 (May 26, 2025 – Sept 29, 2025), ~22 hrs/week assignment load, supervisor: Steve.
