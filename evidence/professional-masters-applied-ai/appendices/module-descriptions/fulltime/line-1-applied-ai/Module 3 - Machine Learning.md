# Module 3: Machine Learning (Full Time)

**Code:** 03.AI | **Learning Line:** Applied Artificial Intelligence | **ECs:** 9

This module develops your understanding of supervised and unsupervised machine learning methods and their application in professional contexts, beginning with regression and generalised linear models, then moving into decision trees, boosting methods, and support vector machines for classification/prediction tasks.

The module also introduces unsupervised learning (clustering, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction), and teaches evaluation of models on predictive performance, cost-efficiency, feasibility, interpretability, and transparency, along with limitations and potential biases. The module concludes with a hands-on project designing, implementing, and evaluating ML models. Continues PAC support via group supervision session: Dealing with Different Audiences.

## Connection with Applied Research

Critical reflection on assumptions, data quality, and model transparency links directly to first- and second-person inquiry, where values, biases, and relational dynamics are examined — integrating technical modelling with inquiry-based reflection.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Machine Learning Modelling** — Analyse the foundations of regression models; apply generalised linear models for regression/classification; apply supervised ML algorithms to complex problems; evaluate objectives/rationales/implications of ML methods in professional contexts; develop unsupervised learning models (clustering, anomaly detection, dimensionality reduction); evaluate interpretability/transparency and identify limitations/biases; assess ML algorithms as business solutions (predictive performance, cost-efficiency, feasibility).

**Research Methodology for Professional Practice** — Transfer knowledge generated through action research to one's own professional practice and wider contexts (linked with PAC supervision).
