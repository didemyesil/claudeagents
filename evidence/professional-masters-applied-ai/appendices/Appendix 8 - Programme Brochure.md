# Professional Master's in Applied Artificial Intelligence — Programme Brochure

Prepare for the hottest job in the AI age by mastering the skills in artificial intelligence and practitioner research.

## The Programme at a Glance

- **Programme Title:** Professional Master's in Applied Artificial Intelligence
- **Duration:** 1 year (Full-time, on campus) & 1.5 years (Part-time, Online)
- **Start Date:** October
- **Credits:** 60 ECTS
- **Workload:** ~40 hours/week (Full-time) & ~20 hours/week (Part-time)

## Programme Details

### Why Applied Artificial Intelligence?

The immense volumes of data and rapidly advancing AI technologies are now taking over the world and changing the way we understand business, people and their behaviours. Organisations from the sciences to healthcare, from finance to the Internet, are trying to make sense of AI technologies and data science, and are on the lookout for skilled people to help lead their business.

Roles like Artificial Intelligence Engineer and Machine-Learning Engineer are consistently highlighted by LinkedIn as among the fastest-growing and most in-demand globally. The World Economic Forum's 2023 Future of Jobs Report projects a 40% net increase in demand for AI & ML specialists and a 30% increase for Data Analysts and Scientists by 2027. Dutch labour-market data (CBS, AI Monitor 2024) shows a sustained elevation in AI-specialist demand and a structural shortage concentrated at the advanced-skills tier. Organisations cite a lack of applied AI experience as the primary barrier to adoption.

### The Skill Set You Will Acquire — Applied AI Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Business Problem Framing** — Frame the business problem clearly using available data and contextual information; formulate a scalable AI solution approach for the identified business problem.

**Exploratory Data Analytics** — Conduct exploratory data analysis to detect patterns, anomalies, and data quality issues; design visualisations that effectively communicate insights to both technical and non-technical audiences; formulate strategic recommendations based on thorough evaluation and synthesis of complex data findings; apply preprocessing steps (scaling, encoding, imputation) to raw data; generate new features using mathematical transformations, interactions, or aggregations; implement feature extraction techniques (e.g., PCA, TF-IDF, embeddings) on sample datasets.

**Machine Learning Modelling** — Analyse the foundations of regression models to evaluate their suitability for solving problems; apply generalised linear models to conduct regression and classification analyses; apply supervised machine learning algorithms to solve complex problems; evaluate the objectives, rationales, and implications of applying machine learning methods in professional contexts; develop unsupervised learning models addressing complex problems such as clustering, anomaly detection, and dimensionality reduction; evaluate the interpretability and transparency of machine learning models, identifying limitations and potential biases; assess ML algorithms as business solutions by evaluating predictive performance, cost-efficiency, and practical feasibility in real-world business contexts.

**Deep Learning** — Analyse the mathematical foundations and underlying logic of neural networks; develop neural networks to address complex problems; design advanced neural network architectures to solve business problems; justify the selection of deep learning architectures by comparing them with traditional machine learning models.

**Computer Vision** — Justify the need for advanced methods in computer vision, depending on the nature of the data (structured vs unstructured); develop deep learning architectures to address complex computer vision problems.

**Responsible AI** — Evaluate ethical responsibilities in data-driven projects from social, scientific, and professional perspectives; identify potential biases and limitations in data sets; assess the ethical implications of using data in AI systems; justify data ownership rights and responsibilities when training models; evaluate the transparency and accountability of AI systems in professional contexts.

**Natural Language Processing** — Transform unstructured text data into structured formats for machine learning applications; apply transformer architectures to solve complex NLP problems; interpret the outcomes of transformer-based NLP models using explainability methods.

**Large Language Models** — Implement pre-trained transformer models from Hugging Face into workflows; fine-tune transformer models for domain-specific challenges; evaluate the necessity of advanced LLMs vs traditional NLP methods.

**Retrieval Augmented Generation Implementation** — Integrate the retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) technique to develop your own language model.

**Machine Learning Operations in Cloud** — Deploy models in cloud environments to support scalable business applications; monitor model performance using A/B testing to inform deployment decisions; detect data drift in production environments and evaluate its impact on model reliability; retrain machine learning models in response to performance degradation while ensuring scalability and efficiency.

### Applied Research Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Self-Leadership** — Ability to reflect on professional practice with a personal leadership perspective to improve personal impact. Use inquiry tools to critically reflect on how personal values, beliefs, and habits impact professional practice; develop a personal leadership perspective using insights from action research, peer dialogue, and relevant leadership theories.

**Interpersonal Leadership** — Ability to engage in group dynamics to achieve desired objectives. Engage in one-to-one or group conversations using appropriate communication tools to achieve results; critically reflect on interpersonal experience as a facilitator in group settings.

**Organisational Leadership** — Ability to evaluate the impact of professional practice in an organisational setting. Undertake an organisational intervention to address a solution in a professional context; evaluate the outcomes of the intervention using an action research approach with attention to wider organisational impact.

**Research Methodology for Professional Practice** — Ability to design and conduct practitioner research using action research methodology to generate actionable knowledge for professional improvement and organisational impact. Apply action research methodology with qualitative and participatory inquiry approaches to design interventions in professional contexts; transfer knowledge generated through action research to one's own professional practice and wider contexts.

## Curriculum

**Foundational Module:** Introduction to the Programme, Community, Action Research. Designed for learners who do not fully meet the basic entry knowledge level or who would like to refresh their skills; gives a gentle introduction to programming thinking and the computational/statistical background needed to complete the programme.

**Certificate 1: AI Foundations & Self-Leadership Inquiry**
- Module 0: Welcome and Orientation — Introduction to Programme & Faculty & Expectations, Community Building + Values, access to orientation module on Canvas, Introduction to Inquiry Mindset / Action Research I
- Module 1: From Business Problem to Data Insights — Understanding the Business Problem, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), Advanced Visualisation, Feature Engineering
- Module 2: Self-Leadership Inquiry — Action Research III (Methodology in Action Research), Inquiry into Values/Purpose/Aspirations/Ethics, Peer Inquiry to Explore Leadership Patterns (VLI), Designing a Self-Directed Learning Path, Reframing Self-Leadership (integrating inquiry insights and transformational theory)
- Module 3: Machine Learning — Regression Analysis + Generalised Linear Models, Tree-based Models & Hyperparameter tuning, Boosting Models & SVM, Clustering, Dimensionality Reduction, Interpretability and Transparency

**Certificate 2: Advanced AI & Interpersonal Leadership Inquiry**
- Module 4: Interpersonal Leadership Inquiry — Action Research III methodology, fields of management/organisational theory, Gestalt/Relational/Psychodynamic approaches, creativity in one-to-one and group settings
- Module 5: Deep Learning — Deep Learning I & II, Computer Vision
- Module 6: Large Language Models — NLP, Transformers Architecture + Hugging Face, LLMs, Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)

**Certificate 3: Advanced AI Technologies and Organisational Leadership Inquiry**
- Module 7: Organisational Leadership Inquiry — Action Research IV (Designing and Evaluating Organisational AR Interventions), creating organisational impact through interventions, Conversing as Organising, the EngageForward Process (Framing/Engaging, Reflecting/Mobilising/Sustaining)
- Module 8: Responsible AI — Fairness and Non-Discrimination, Privacy and Data Protection, Transparency and Accountability
- Module 9: ML Ops — Model Deployment in Cloud, Model Performance Monitoring (A/B Testing), Data Drift, Re-training the Models

**Final Project and Supervision** — Design and execute a work-based project using the Action Research Methodology. Full-time: supervision begins at the start of the programme and runs alongside modules; report submitted at end of Year 1. Part-time: supervision begins after completing the three certificates; report submitted at end of Year 1.5.

## The Learner Experience

**Flexible and Innovative** — designed to fit the needs of a 21st-century learner.

**Interactive and Community-based** — part of a community of experts, supported by facilitators, coaches, supervisors and programme managers.

**Holistic and Practice-based** — technical skills combined with leadership skills and a business mindset; weekly assignments and projects, individually and with peers.

**Cornerstones of the Team Academy Learner Experience:**
- *Digital Learning* — asynchronous digital content via the digital Campus platform (readings, videos, practical exercises).
- *Workshops* — interactive, practice-oriented, facilitator-guided real-time challenges and collaborative problem-solving.
- *Team Coaching* — dialogical, experiential process supporting collective learning and performance through weekly group sessions.
- *Research Supervision Sessions* — structured academic/professional guidance for practitioner research using action research methodology; in the full-time programme this runs in parallel with modules from the start.
- *Assignments and Projects* — weekly assignments tied to module competencies, module projects, and integrated projects at the end of each certification.
- *Reflection and Feedback* — continuous formative feedback via rubrics, learning journals, and dialogue-based tools.
- *Town Halls* — bi-monthly community events hosted by programme managers.
- *VIVAs* — individual sessions demonstrating learning mastery through portfolio review at the end of each certification, a high-stakes progress decision by an assessor panel; mini VIVAs also held during the final project phase.

**Talent Accelerator Programme** — voluntary programme supporting career identity and visibility, including 1-1 career coaching, career guidance on the job market, and CV enhancement & personal branding.

## Admissions

**Who should apply?** — Those wanting to start a new career in AI and data science, upgrade skills and employability for data science/AI jobs, or work as a freelance AI/data scientist or start their own business. Applications are evaluated holistically (educational background, professional experience, motivation). Most successful candidates share: a Bachelor's degree; English proficiency at C1 level; motivation and ~20 hrs/week (part-time) or ~40 hrs/week (full-time) commitment.

**The Admission Process:**
1. **Basic Entry Requirements** — Bachelor's degree (accredited institution); English proficiency C1 (IELTS/TOEFL/equivalent); demonstrable motivation and commitment.
2. **Application Submission** — online application form, academic diplomas, relevant certifications (if available), updated CV.
3. **Initial Intake Interview** — Admissions Consultant assesses motivation/fit, communication skills, and verifies evidence of intermediate-level programming skills (preferably Python) and fundamental mathematics/statistics knowledge.
4. **Enrolment in preparatory module (if needed)** — a structured foundations module (Module 0), or a proficiency assessment (online test covering basic Python, mathematics/statistics fundamentals, leadership awareness, plus a follow-up call with faculty) to waive it.
5. **Decision & Recommendations** — key faculty decide on Admission to the Main Programme or Conditional Admission (enrolment in the Preparatory programme to bridge skill gaps).

**Preparatory Module (Module 0) as a Bridge for Academic Readiness** — ensures the programme remains accessible while safeguarding NLQF 7/master's-level rigour. Functions as a bridging programme for candidates needing reinforcement in Python programming, statistical thinking, and leadership awareness. Delivered over nine weeks, flexible/online/self-paced, ~50 hours of targeted learning with weekly live group mentoring: coding skills (control flows, data structures, functions, Git workflows), probability and hypothesis testing, initial leadership sensitivity (leadership types, power relations in organisations). Completion of the preparatory project is required for progression into the main programme.
