# Programme Outcomes of the Professional Master's in Applied Artificial Intelligence

The programme outcomes of the Professional Master's in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Team Academy are based on the current developments in AI, leadership, and practitioner research. They are shaped in close dialogue with the professional field to ensure that the curriculum remains responsive to the rapidly evolving AI landscape and the needs of society. In our view, a Professional Master's in Applied AI must enable graduates to work autonomously and responsibly on complex, real-world challenges, using advanced AI methods alongside reflective leadership and cycles of inquiry to create meaningful solutions.

The curriculum, therefore, integrates technical expertise such as machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, computer vision, responsible AI, and MLOps, with action research methodology and its inquiry-based tools at the self, group and organisational levels. Through this integration, students not only gain mastery of applied AI techniques but also learn to apply them responsibly, collaboratively, and with impact. This holistic approach aligns with the Dutch Qualifications Framework (NLQF) and Dublin Descriptors at level 7. Thus, the programme outcomes are identified as follows:

**1. Mastery**
Graduates demonstrate mastery of Applied AI by acting autonomously and ethically in complex and uncertain contexts, reflecting critically on their personal leadership practice, and integrating responsible AI principles into professional decision-making.

**2. Research Competence**
Graduates are able to design and conduct practitioner research that frames business problems into AI solutions, analyse and model data with advanced techniques, and generate insights through cycles of inquiry and reflection, which contributes to fresh thinking and applications in their professional practice.

**3. Interprofessional Conduct**
Graduates collaborate effectively across disciplines and organisational levels, facilitating dialogue and interventions in diverse professional and cultural contexts, and communicating complex AI concepts and research outcomes clearly to both specialist and non-specialist audiences.

**4. Effect**
Graduates deliver measurable impact in their professional practice by deploying AI systems in organisational settings with a sense of responsibility, transferring knowledge across domains, and helping to shape future-oriented strategies that have an impact on their work and communities.
