# Module 1: From Business Problem to Data Insights (Full Time)

**Code:** 01.AI | **Learning Line:** Applied Artificial Intelligence | **ECs:** 6

This module introduces the foundations of applied AI by focusing on how professional challenges are translated into data-driven inquiries. You will learn to frame business problems using contextual information and available data, and to design scalable AI solution approaches that align with organisational goals.

The module then develops your ability to explore and interpret data systematically: conducting exploratory data analysis to detect patterns, anomalies, and data quality issues; designing visualisations that communicate insights effectively to both technical and non-technical audiences; feature engineering; and formulating strategic recommendations. The module concludes with a project applying these techniques to a realistic case. This module also begins supporting Professional Academic Communication (PAC) through group supervision Session I: Introduction to Professional Academic Communication.

## Connection with Applied Research

Although part of the Applied AI line, this module is closely connected with the Applied Research line. The inquiry mindset introduced at the start of the programme carries into technical practice here: framing business problems, exploring data, and communicating insights all involve questioning assumptions, considering context, and reflecting on how choices shape outcomes.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Business Problem Framing** — Frame the business problem using available data and contextual information; formulate a scalable AI solution approach.

**Exploratory Analytics** — Conduct exploratory data analysis to detect patterns, anomalies, and data quality issues; design visualisations for technical and non-technical audiences; formulate strategic recommendations; apply preprocessing (scaling, encoding, imputation); generate new features via transformations/interactions/aggregations; implement feature extraction (PCA, TF-IDF, embeddings).

**Research Methodology for Professional Practice** — Transfer knowledge generated through action research to one's own professional practice and wider contexts (linked with PAC supervision).

Forms of learning: Self-Study, Peer-Study, Workshops, Team Coaching Sessions, Group Research and PAC Supervision, Assignments and Projects.

## Assessment

Follows the programmatic assessment model: multiple low- and medium-stake tasks generate evidence toward the Certificate 1 portfolio, formally reviewed by a panel in combination with the VIVA session (high-stake). Per learning outcome: at least 3 low-stake, 2 medium-stake, and 1 high-stake evidence items.

- **Low-Stake:** Weekly Assignments (Canvas submissions); Workshop Exercises (uploaded artefacts); Team Coaching Reflections (reflection posts).
- **Medium-Stake:** Module Project (end-of-module project output); End of Certification Group Project (comprehensive integrated group project, at certificate end only).
- **High-Stake:** At the end of Certification 1, all evidence (reflections, inquiry artefacts, leadership essay) is curated into the learning portfolio, reviewed with the VIVA (oral evaluation). Progression is holistic per certificate, not pass/fail per module.

**Progression rules:** each certification requires mastery at or above "Sufficient" for all included LOs. If one LO falls below Sufficient and isn't a prerequisite, progression continues with revise/resubmit within a set window. If two or more LOs are below Sufficient, progression continues with a remediation plan. Repeated/unresolved insufficiencies may lead to a No-Progress / Repeat or exit decision.

**Mastery Scale (0–3):** No Evidence — Below Sufficient — Sufficient (Pass Threshold) — Good. Each evidence item has specific criteria/descriptors on Canvas. Assessors give open-ended feedback answering: "What does your submitted evidence currently demonstrate about your progress?" and "What is the most important next step to strengthen your development in this learning outcome?" Canvas auto-calculates the learning mastery score per learning outcome (visible under Grades).
