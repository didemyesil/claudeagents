# Faculty Roles in the Bachelor's in Applied Computing

This document translates our values and pedagogical philosophy into profiles for the key roles held by faculty and staff at Team Academy's Bachelor of Science in Applied Computing. As a professional, practice-based programme aligned with Dutch bachelor's standards (NLQF Level 6), faculty are not traditional lecturers but facilitators, coaches, supervisors and assessors who guide learners in developing technical mastery, leadership and action inquiry capacity.

## 1. The Facilitator

Leads workshops, activating/supporting participatory learning aligned with the competency-based model; designs/delivers interactive sessions (hands-on coding, discussion, peer exchange, inquiry-based learning); acts as co-creator of learning environments rather than a lecturer.

Responsibilities: prepare/deliver workshops aligned with competencies, learning outcomes and learners' needs; guide theory-to-practice application; foster a collaborative learning culture; facilitate critical reflection/dialogue/group problem-solving; contribute to module development via feedback; collaborate with coaches/supervisors; maintain accessibility/inclusion/academic integrity standards; support formative assessment with developmental feedback; encourage learner autonomy within structure.

## 3. Team Coach

Supports the learning community via group dialogue, reflective practice, interpersonal development; creates a safe, challenging, collaborative environment; acts as a process facilitator (not a traditional academic instructor) for shared responsibility, self-directed learning, and relational competencies underpinning the leadership and Action Inquiry learning lines.

Responsibilities: facilitate reflective/peer-learning/sense-making group sessions; support leadership competency development (self-awareness, interpersonal awareness, responsible participation, aligned with the Action Inquiry Line); hold space for constructive dialogue psychologically safely; guide personal/collective learning intentions and progress review; observe/address group dynamics for healthy communication/trust/accountability; connect discussions to competencies/outcomes; collaborate with faculty/programme managers, flagging engagement/participation/well-being concerns; provide developmental, process-focused feedback; participate in internal calibration/reflection/development; maintain professional boundaries, ethical conduct, confidentiality, alignment with organisational leadership philosophy.

## 4. Assessor

Appointed by the Examination Board; designs, develops, administers, and grades assessments across bachelor's modules, aligned with the Assessment Policy and learning outcomes.

Responsibilities: design valid/reliable/transparent assessments via constructive alignment; develop assignments/projects/presentations/rubrics; administer per ethical/integrity standards; evaluate fairly with predefined rubrics; provide clear actionable feedback/feedforward; analyse/refine assessments based on data/stakeholder input; comply with the 4-Eye Policy and Examination Board guidelines; communicate protocols clearly; participate in QA processes; uphold assessment ethics (objectivity, fairness, impartiality); possess sufficient subject-matter/linguistic proficiency.

## 5. Supervisor

Guides learners through the Final Project — designing/executing a project integrating technical skills, leadership, and applied research/action inquiry per programme outcomes and NLQF Level 6 standards; facilitates reflection, provides structured feedback, ensures rigour/relevance/integrity; enables well-reasoned, ethical, autonomous learner decisions rather than providing solutions.

Responsibilities: support topic/approach scoping aligned with competencies/assessment criteria; give timely constructive draft feedback promoting clarity/coherence/critical thinking; guide academic integrity/ethical awareness/responsible leadership; monitor progress via regular supervision check-ins; liaise with the programme manager on concerns; contribute to internal QA by reflecting on supervision practice.

## Competency Profile for Faculty Members

- **Interpersonal Skills** — care, courage, collaboration; open/respectful communication; trust via active listening/presence/integrity; inclusive spaces.
- **Commitment to Growth** — accountability/courage in professional development; responsive to domain shifts; continuous reflection/evolution of practice.
- **Curriculum Development** — actively contribute to engaging, learner-centred curricula; active curriculum-team membership; keep programmes current with academic/industry standards.
- **Facilitative Teaching & Supervision** — active/participatory methods nurturing critical thinking, real-world problem solving, ethical leadership; guide through ambiguity/complexity while supporting autonomy/voice.
- **Competency-Based Assessment & Feedback** — transparent/purposeful/developmental assessments; feedback promoting self-awareness/learning ownership/leadership growth; reflect both academic mastery and reflective practice/ethical reasoning.
- **Contribution to the Learning Culture** — shape/uphold shared responsibility and participation; bring voice to organisational development; honest reflection/dialogue to improve systems/policies/practices.
- **Mentorship & Leadership Practice** — model responsible/relational leadership; coach colleagues with humility/respect; share experience generously; hold space for others' growth; contribute to a trust/learning/collective-impact leadership culture.

## Selection & Hiring Criteria

- At least a master's degree in a relevant field (Software Engineering, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, Educational Leadership, Applied Research)
- Relevant industry experience enabling practical translation of concepts
- Higher-education teaching and assessment experience
- Strong command of English (C1 or above)
- Commitment to ongoing professional development and learning agility
- Understanding of and alignment with the Team Academy leadership manifesto and learning philosophy
- Cultural competence and ability to foster inclusive learning environments
