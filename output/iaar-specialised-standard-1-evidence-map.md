### Genuine gaps — resolved by Didem, ready for Stage 2

All four gaps below were open questions when this map was first produced.
Didem has now answered all four; they are kept in this section (relabelled)

- **Gender equality / equal opportunities policy statement** — satisfies:
  Guidelines req "guards against intolerance or discrimination"; Sample Q
  14 ("Does the EO have a gender equality/equal opportunities concept?").
  — blocks: shared/institution-wide content (this would logically be one
  institution-wide statement, not three per-programme copies, per the same
  shared-evidence logic used elsewhere in this standard). Searched the
  full evidence pool for "gender," "equal opportunit*," "discrimination
  policy," "diversity policy," "inclusion policy" — the only hits are
  scattered mentions inside assessment documents (accommodations for
  learners with disabilities "promoting equal opportunities for success";
  non-discriminatory admissions handling in the QA Policy's §1.4; diversity
  criteria for Field Representative selection). None of these amount to a
  standalone gender-equality/equal-opportunities policy statement or
  concept the way Sample Q 14 asks for. **Specific question for Didem:**
  Does Team Academy have (or intend to formally adopt before this
  application) a written gender-equality/equal-opportunities statement —
  even a short one — covering staff and student treatment institution-wide?
  If yes, where is it and can it be added to `_shared/`; if not, does she
  want the Folder Developer to draft one from scratch for her review
  (grounded in the non-discriminatory-admissions and inclusive-assessment
  language already on file), or would she rather answer Sample Q 14 by
  pointing to the existing scattered provisions and stating a formal
  standalone policy is planned?
  **Resolved:** Didem confirmed the Folder Developer should draft this —
  grounded in the non-discriminatory-admissions and inclusive-assessment
  language already on file (Policy on Assessment, TER, Field Representative
  diversity criteria), not invented from nothing. Draft a short, shared,
  institution-wide statement, one copy for the whole cluster, and route it
  back to Didem for review once drafted (this is Case-3 "draft new" per
  `folder-developer`'s three-case rule, not a remaining open question).

- **QA policy revision-history / stakeholder-satisfaction assessment
  mechanism, explicitly named as such** — satisfies: Guidelines req "how
  the policy is implemented, monitored and revised is the institution's
  own decision"; Sample Qs 5 (how did the QA policy change over time), 6
  (results of assessing stakeholder satisfaction with the QA policy). —
  blocks: shared/institution-wide content. Per the ex-ante note carried
  forward from the config and the checklist's own Standard 1 note, actual
  historical revision data and actual satisfaction-survey results are
  correctly **not** expected pre-launch — this is not a fabrication
  opportunity. What is still missing, though, is an explicit **planned
  mechanism** statement: the QA Policy document describes an ongoing PDCA
  evaluation process for the QA system in general (§1.1.5) but does not
  say, specifically, how/when the QA *policy document itself* (as opposed
  to programmes, modules, activities) will be periodically reviewed and by
  whom, or how stakeholder satisfaction with the *policy* specifically
  (not just programme-level satisfaction data, which §1.1.7/§ESG 1.7
  already covers) will be assessed once the programmes are running.
  **Specific question for Didem:** Is there an intended review cycle for
  the QA Policy document itself (e.g. annual review by the QA Team with
  Executive Board sign-off), and should stakeholder satisfaction with the
  QA policy specifically be folded into the existing learner-survey/
  performance-review mechanisms already described, or does she want a
  distinct line added? This can likely be answered with one or two
  sentences added to §1.1.5 of the shared QA Policy rather than a new
  document — flagging it so the Folder Developer doesn't invent a review
  cadence unprompted.
  **Resolved:** Didem confirmed this goes inside the QA Policy document
  itself (§1.1.5, alongside the existing PDCA evaluation description) —
  not a separate document. Folder Developer: adapt §1.1.5 to add an
  explicit line naming the QA-policy-specific review cycle and how
  stakeholder satisfaction with the policy itself will be assessed once
  programmes are running, framed as a planned mechanism (ex-ante), not
  historical data. Shared, one edit to the one shared document.

  Guidelines requirement on academic integrity/non-discrimination need

  Assessment Policy/Exam Board Handbook are programme-specific regulatory

  reuse the same regulatory framework; if Tech MBA runs under a materially
  different assessment/examination model, that needs to be described from
  scratch and grounded in facts from her, not assumed.

  Examination Board Handbook will be **shared with the Master's** — i.e.
  Tech MBA reuses/adapts the Master's regulatory framework rather than

  "genuine gap" to a **Case-2 adaptation task**: Folder Developer adapts

  Teaching and Examinations Regulations.md`, and `Appendix 15 - Policy on
  Assessment.md` into Tech MBA-named copies, the same operation already
