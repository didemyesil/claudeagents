# Equal Opportunities Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all programmes of Schoolvision and to everyone in its community: applicants, learners, alumni, faculty, staff and partners. It forms part of the quality assurance system and supports the Policy for Quality Assurance.

## Commitment

All applicants from diverse backgrounds, cultures, genders and nationalities regardless of their physical disability are encouraged to apply to Schoolvision programmes. Schoolvision is committed in its pursuit of academic excellence to equality of opportunity, which encourages all under-represented groups, promotes a diverse and inclusive culture and community. Schoolvision is also committed to gender equality: learners and staff of all genders have the same opportunities to apply, to study, to work and to progress. This is represented by Schoolvision's core values as stated below:

**Human Potential:** We truly believe in every individual's potential regardless of race, color, gender or physical ability to develop when the right learning environment is created.

**Collaboration:** We know that every individual brings a unique perspective to the table and positive impact is made possible through collaboration.

**Community Learning:** Humans are social beings; therefore, learning is most effective when it happens in a community.

**Transparency:** Being open helps us build trust and integrity among the Schoolvision's community members.

**Innovation:** We constantly explore tools and processes to make learning more relevant in the digital age.

## Admission and employment

Schoolvision is also committed to a policy and a practice which require, for students, the admissions team to accept applications only by personal merit and application requirements. For staff, entry into employment and progression is only determined by the same. The selection of Field Representatives also takes diversity into account.

## Protection from discrimination

No student or staff member is treated less favourably than another because of her or his belonging to a protected class or group. This applies to a former student or alumna, faculty or an employer partner in the Schoolvision's community. Schoolvision does not tolerate intolerance of any kind, or discrimination, harassment or victimization of learners or staff.

If any person admitted as a student, faculty member or a partner considers that he or she is suffering from any kind of discrimination, harassment or victimization because of belonging, he or she may make a complaint which will be dealt with through the agreed procedures for complaints.

## Responsibility and review

The QA Team monitors the application of this policy. The Executive Board approves it and any change to it. The policy is reviewed annually together with the Policy for Quality Assurance, and it is available to learners, staff and partners together with that policy.
