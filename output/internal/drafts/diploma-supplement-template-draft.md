# DIPLOMA SUPPLEMENT

*This Diploma Supplement model was developed by the European Commission, the Council of Europe and UNESCO. The purpose of the supplement is to provide sufficient independent data to improve the international transparency and fair academic and professional recognition of qualifications (diplomas, degrees, certificates etc.). It is designed to provide a description of the nature, level, context, content and status of the studies that were pursued and successfully completed by the individual named on the original qualification to which this supplement is appended. It is free from any value judgements, equivalence statements or suggestions about recognition.*

## 8. INFORMATION ON THE NATIONAL HIGHER EDUCATION SYSTEM

Higher education in the Netherlands has a binary system: research-oriented education at research universities (wo) and professionally oriented education at universities of applied sciences (hbo). Both follow a three-cycle bachelor–master–doctorate structure and use the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS). One credit represents 28 hours of study, and one year of full-time study comprises 60 credits.

Professionally oriented bachelor's programmes comprise 240 credits and lead to level 6 of the Dutch Qualifications Framework (NLQF), which corresponds to the first cycle of the Framework for Qualifications of the European Higher Education Area (QF-EHEA). Professionally oriented master's programmes comprise 60 to 120 credits and lead to NLQF level 7, which corresponds to the second cycle of the QF-EHEA. A master's degree gives access to doctoral programmes, subject to the admission requirements of the institution concerned.

The Accreditation Organisation of the Netherlands and Flanders (NVAO) is responsible for the accreditation of higher education programmes in the Netherlands.
