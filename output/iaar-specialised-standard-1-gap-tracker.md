## Consistency

- No internal contradiction found within the SAR section itself — the
  self-assessed position ("Satisfactory," not "Strong") is consistent with
  the body text's own acknowledgement that the Bachelor's stakeholder
  evidence is not yet matched by Master's/Tech MBA.
- **Finding (moderate) — an unflagged factual inconsistency in the
  underlying Tech MBA source evidence, inherited silently into the adapted
  TER/Assessment Policy without being surfaced:**
  - `Tech MBA Programme Handbook_2024.md` (line 222) and the adapted
    `evidence/tech-mba/Teaching and Examination Regulations (TER).md`
    (Article 2.1) both state the Final Project is **560 hours / 20 ECTS**,
    for a programme total of **1680 hours / 60 ECTS**.
  - `Tech MBA Supervision Guide.md` (lines 299, 305) states the Final
    Project is **21.5 ECTS**, with an explicit "(21,5×28) 602 hours" time
    calculation — a different number from the Programme Handbook and the
    adapted TER. At 21.5 ECTS the four-certification total (40 ECTS) plus
    Final Project would sum to 61.5 ECTS, not the stated 60 ECTS total.
  - The two Tech MBA source documents also **name the fourth certification
    differently**: `Tech MBA Programme Handbook_2024.md` (line 221) and the
    adapted TER/Assessment Policy both use **"Leadership and Action
    Research,"** while `Tech MBA Supervision Guide.md` (line 295) calls the
    same certification **"Transformative Leadership Certification."**
  - This is a pre-existing conflict between two of Team Academy's own
    source documents, not something Stage 2 invented — Stage 2's Tech MBA
    TER/Assessment Policy consistently used the Programme Handbook's
    numbers and did not fabricate anything. But the adapted TER's own
    closing note states "No fact in this document was invented," which,
    while true, does not disclose that a *conflicting* fact exists
    elsewhere in the evidence pool. A panel member who cross-checks the
    adapted TER against the Supervision Guide (the more detailed document
    on Final Project mechanics) will find this discrepancy immediately.
    This should be resolved with Didem — which of the two figures/names is
    correct — before Standard 1 (or any later standard reusing these
    documents, e.g. Standards 3–4) is finalised. Not severe enough alone
    to block Stage 4 on its own, but it is a real, findable defect and
    should be logged as an open item (see Open Questions below), not left
    for a panel member to discover first.
- No other cross-section terminology drift found (e.g. "Examination Board,"
  "Field Representatives," "Programme Manager," "PDCA cycle," "ESG"
  section numbering are used consistently throughout the SAR section and
  the evidence documents it cites).

## Overall assessment

- **Result: REVISION NEEDED — must return to Stage 2 (`folder-developer`)**,
  narrowly scoped.

The draft is substantively strong: every check on completeness,
traceability of claims, voice, and the cluster shared/per-programme
judgment passed, and Stage 2's discipline against fabrication held up
under direct source-checking across all 10 evidence documents. The two
open items the draft itself already flags (Gender Equality sign-off,
subcontracting) are properly and visibly surfaced, not silently dropped,
and do not by themselves require sending this back — under the agent
definition's flow rule they could ride along as open items into Stage 4.

However, this audit found one item the draft did **not** surface: a
genuine internal conflict between two of Team Academy's own Tech MBA
source documents (Programme Handbook vs. Supervision Guide) on the Final
Project's ECTS/hours and the fourth certification's name, which the
adapted Tech MBA TER/Assessment Policy resolved silently in one direction.
Per the flow rule, an unflagged consistency defect — even one inherited
from the source evidence rather than invented by Stage 2 — means the file
is not yet genuinely ready and does not go to Stage 4 as-is.

**Required fix before re-submission (small, targeted):**
- Add this conflict as a third item under "Open questions for Didem" in
  the SAR section (parallel to how the Gender Equality Statement and
  subcontracting items are already handled), and/or resolve it directly
  with Didem and correct the Tech MBA TER/Assessment Policy/Examination
  Board Handbook's Article 2.1 table and certification name if the
  Supervision Guide's figures turn out to be the correct ones.
- No other change is required. This is not a request to re-draft the
  section; a targeted patch resolving/flagging this one item is
  sufficient to return to Stage 3 for a quick re-check, or — since the fix
  is narrow and well-defined — Didem may choose to resolve it directly and
  let Stage 2 apply a one-line fix without a full re-audit cycle.

### Process feedback (secondary, does not affect the verdict)

Stage 2's own "Process feedback" section in the draft is good and worth
Didem's attention when scaling to Standards 2–10 (especially point 3 on
Stage 1.5 pre-flagging shared/regulatory vs. programme-specific content in
large documents, and point 6 on a per-programme "known facts inventory").
One addition from this audit: **Stage 1.5 (or Stage 2) should
cross-check multiple source documents for the same underlying fact
(e.g. ECTS/credit tables) when more than one source document exists for
a programme**, since this is exactly how the Tech MBA discrepancy above
arose — the Programme Handbook and Supervision Guide were each read and
trusted individually rather than checked against each other. This is a
generally applicable risk (Standards 3–4 will reuse these same Tech MBA
documents) and worth a one-line addition to the Stage 1.5 or Stage 2
agent instructions.
