# Module 6: Team Inquiry in Professional Practice

**Year:** 3 | **Code:** 06.RP | **Learning Line:** Research Practice Line | **ECs:** 7.5

Shared across all three tracks. Third stage of the Research Practice Line, building on self- and one-to-one inquiry, shifting focus to collective learning and shared sense-making in teams. Applies Plan–Act–Observe–Reflect to a team challenge: gather perspectives, use structured dialogue to understand patterns/solutions, test a collective action, observe effects, reflect on team learning.

## Connection with the Professional Practice Line

Practised through authentic challenges from technical projects, agile teamwork, traineeship-related contexts, stakeholder collaboration, quality improvement, professional decision-making. Foundation for stakeholder engagement, software development process management, applied research, and the final project.

## Intended Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Team inquiry** — gain team insights to understand professional challenges/solutions; use team dialogue and reflection to support shared problem solving in a work environment.

## Module Planning (5 weeks + seasonal application)

Week 1: From One-to-one Inquiry to Team Inquiry. Week 2: Team Observation and Multiple Perspectives. Week 3: Team Dialogue and Collective Sense-making. Week 4: Shared Problem Solving and Team Reflection. Week 5: Integrated Team Inquiry Cycle and Module Project.

**Seasonal Application:** continues through Season 3's agile rhythm and authentic work-environment contexts (team challenges, project decisions, quality issues, collaboration patterns, stakeholder feedback). **Vertical Leadership Integration** (Season 3 focus): multiple-perspective inquiry, collective sense-making, constructive team dialogue, shared problem solving, collective accountability.

## Weekly Study Load (41 hrs total)

Team inquiry practice/Module Project (blended): 25 | Self-study (online): 4 | Workshops (online): 2 | Standups (campus): 2 | Squads (campus): 4 | Team Coaching (campus): 4

## Assessment

**Low-stake:** workshop/sprint reflections, standup participation, squad participation, team coaching contributions.
**Medium-stake:** Module Project (one complete team inquiry cycle — team context/focus, multiple perspectives, observations, dialogue evidence, collective action, outcomes, reflection, improvement decisions, synthesis).
**High-stake:** Certification VIVA (end of Certification 3).

**Passing criteria:** ≥75% low-stake, ≥75% medium-stake, Certification VIVA passed.
