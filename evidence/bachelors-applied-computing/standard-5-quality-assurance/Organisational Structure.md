## Role Descriptions

**The Executive Board** provides overall governance and strategic direction for Team Academy, including the Bachelor's in Applied Computing.

**The Examination Board** is responsible for ensuring the quality and integrity of assessment processes, overseeing examination standards, and safeguarding the academic quality of the programme and the level of the diploma — see the Bachelor's Examination Board Handbook (`standard-3-assessment/Examination Board Handbook.md`).

**QA Team** provides quality assurance oversight, ensuring academic standards and quality measures are maintained for the Bachelor's programme, per the institution-wide QA Policy.

**The Educational Council (Student Advisory Board)** consists of student representatives, including Bachelor's learners, who share their experience of programme quality and provide advisory input. Their recommendations are passed to the Managing Director, who shares them with the Programme Manager(s) and Programme Design Leads.

**Managing Director** oversees operational management of Team Academy, coordinating between departments and implementing strategic decisions from the Executive Board.

**Programme Manager(s)** manage the Bachelor's programme's administration and coordination between faculty, students and the programme design lead.

**Programme Design & Development Lead** is responsible for designing and developing the Bachelor's curriculum (across its three tracks — Applied Software Engineering, Applied Machine Learning, Applied Data Science), ensuring curriculum quality and improvement.

**Faculty** are the academic staff who design and deliver the Bachelor's learning activities, design and develop assessments as part of the curriculum team, and evaluate assessments.

**Head Coach** supervises and coordinates the team coaching function for the Bachelor's, working directly with Team Coaches.

**Team Coaches** provide coaching support to student teams' learning.

**Admissions Manager** handles Bachelor's admissions processes.

**Finance** manages financial operations, budgeting, and financial reporting institution-wide.

**Marketing** is responsible for marketing activities and external communications, including for the Bachelor's.

**Web Development** maintains Team Academy's web presence.
