# Module 8: Responsible AI (Full Time)

**Code:** 08.AI | **Learning Line:** Applied Artificial Intelligence | **ECs:** 4

Develops your ability to approach AI projects through responsibility and ethics: evaluating fairness and non-discrimination in algorithmic systems; analysing biases and limitations in datasets; privacy and data protection (rights, responsibilities, compliance); transparency and accountability, and their effect on trustworthiness/acceptance of AI systems. A practical project brings these strands together, designing and evaluating AI solutions with both technical performance and ethical integrity.

## Connection with Applied Research

First-person inquiry supports awareness of personal bias when handling data; second-person inquiry relates to how fairness/transparency affect collaboration and communication; third-person inquiry highlights organisational and societal consequences of AI deployment.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Responsible AI** — Evaluate ethical responsibilities in data-driven projects from social, scientific, and professional perspectives; identify potential biases and limitations in data sets; assess the ethical implications of using data in AI systems; justify data ownership rights and responsibilities when training models; evaluate the transparency and accountability of AI systems in professional contexts.
