# Document Scope Register

The decision layer for the whole application. Before any document is produced,
adapted or cited, its scope is settled here. Every later stage obeys this file.

It exists because "shared with the Master's" was once implemented as "produce a
separate Tech MBA copy" — the opposite of the decision. Scope is now recorded
as a value, not inferred from prose.

## Scope values

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `INSTITUTION` | One document for the whole institution. No per-programme copies. Every programme cites the same document. |
| `MASTERS-LEVEL` | One document covering the Professional Master's in Applied AI and the Tech MBA. Provisions common to both are stated once; provisions for one only sit in a programme-specific annex at the end. |
| `BACHELORS` | The Bachelor's in Applied Computing has its own document, reflecting NLQF level 6 entry, progression and graduation rules. |
| `PROGRAMME` | Genuinely one per programme — the content differs by its nature. |

## Why the regulatory documents split at level, not per programme

IAAR does not prescribe how an institution structures its internal regulatory
documents. Its Standard 1 asks how far the programmes "comply with the
regulatory documents of the EO" — that is, it assumes institution-level
documents and tests programme conformity against them. Its cluster rule
("aspects common to all programmes are described once") governs the report,
not the document set.

The binding constraint is Dutch: Team Academy is a recognised legal entity for
higher education under the WHW, which requires regulations "for each programme
or group of programmes" — so grouping is expressly permitted. Standard practice
in the Netherlands groups by level: one set for the bachelor's programmes, one
for the master's, each with a common part and a programme-specific annex.
Bachelor-only provisions — the propedeuse, the binding study advice, entry on a
secondary-school diploma — have no master's equivalent, so merging the levels
would fill the document with clauses that apply to half its readers.

Aligning with the national pattern is itself an answer to IAAR, whose Standard 1
requires the policy to take account of the national context.

## Open questions

**Organisational Structure and Field Representative Guidelines.** Both are
institution-wide in substance. Copies were nonetheless produced for the
Bachelor's and the Tech MBA, which now have to be kept in step with the
original. Recommendation: keep one institutional document for each, delete the
copies, and have every standard cite the one document. Not yet decided.

## Rule

A document is produced only after its scope is settled here. Where a scope says
one document covers several programmes, that means **one file** — a copy
bearing another programme's name is a breach of this register, not an
implementation of it.
