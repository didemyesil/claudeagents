# Module 6: Large Language Models (Full Time)

**Code:** 06.AI | **Learning Line:** Applied Artificial Intelligence | **ECs:** 6

Builds advanced capability in NLP and LLMs: transforming unstructured text into structured representations (tokenisation, feature extraction); transformer architectures (training a simple model, fine-tuning pre-trained models via Hugging Face); interpreting model outputs with explainability techniques; LLM training/use/adaptation/deployment; comparing LLM-based methods with traditional NLP; RLHF concepts; retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Culminates in applied development of transformer-based solutions (fine-tuning, evaluation, deployment considerations).

## Connection with Applied Research

Working with LLMs is an inquiry into how knowledge is represented, generated, and communicated — reflecting on assumptions in data, interpretability of outputs, and ethical implications of automating language-based tasks; links to first-, second-, and third-person inquiry.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Natural Language Processing (NLP)** — Transform unstructured text into structured formats for ML applications; apply transformer architectures to complex NLP problems; interpret transformer-based NLP model outcomes via explainability methods.

**Large Language Models (LLM)** — Implement pre-trained transformer models from Hugging Face into workflows; fine-tune transformer models for domain-specific challenges; evaluate the necessity of advanced LLMs vs traditional NLP methods.

**RAG Implementation** — Integrate retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) techniques to develop language models.

Papers: Vaswani et al. (2017) 'Attention is all you need', NeurIPS 30; Brown et al. (2020) 'Language models are few-shot learners', NeurIPS 33; Touvron et al. (2023) 'LLaMA 2: Open foundation and fine-tuned chat models', Meta AI.
