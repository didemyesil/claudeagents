## Biggest gaps to resolve before Stage 2 drafting

Ranked by how much each would block `folder-developer` from producing real (non-fabricated) content:

1. **Bachelor's 240 EC vs 180 EC / missing Year 4 structure (Standard 2, spills into Standard 4).** This is not a documentation gap Stage 2 can draft around — it is an unresolved curriculum-design decision. The institution's own QA review (`Programme Inconsistency and Accuracy Check.md`) and independent field-representative feedback both flag it as unresolved and "Priority." Stage 2 cannot write a coherent Standard 2 (or graduation/certification) section for the Bachelor's until Didem's team formally approves the Year 4 / final-60-EC structure (or the accredited programme volume is redefined). This should be resolved first, ahead of any Stage 2 run touching the Bachelor's.

2. **Tech MBA lacks foundational governance documents across almost every standard.** No QA policy, no TER/formal regulation, no Examination Board, no recruitment policy, no review/monitoring procedure, no public-facing brochure. The curriculum and assessment *content* is genuinely rich (Programme Handbook, Curriculum v10, Supervision Guide are detailed and usable), but the *governance layer* that IAAR's standards actually evaluate is almost entirely missing. This will require either (a) real institutional decisions about who chairs a Tech MBA Examination Board, what its formal TER says, etc., before Stage 2 can draft real (non-invented) content, or (b) an explicit, deliberate decision to adapt the shared institutional QA/TER framework to Tech MBA — which is itself a real decision someone needs to make, not something Stage 2 can infer silently.

3. **Standard 7 (Information Management) has no real system-description document for any programme.** The only content is a bullet list of data categories embedded in the QA policy. Stage 2 cannot adapt what doesn't exist in any usable form — this needs either a short interview/fact-finding pass with whoever owns the LMS/data systems, or an explicit list of real facts (which LMS, what fields, what retention, who reviews it) handed to Stage 2 before drafting, to avoid inventing a system description.

4. **Lisbon Convention / ENIC-NARIC recognition mechanism (Standard 4) is completely absent cluster-wide.** Confirmed by exhaustive search — zero hits anywhere in the evidence pool. This is likely fixable with a real, short institutional policy statement (Team Academy's actual approach to recognising foreign qualifications) rather than a large drafting exercise, but it cannot be drafted from nothing — someone at the institution needs to state what the actual practice is.

5. **Standard 9 evidence exists in strong form only for the Bachelor's.** The Action Planning and Field Representative Feedback documents are exactly the kind of ex-ante-appropriate evidence IAAR wants, but nothing equivalent exists for the Master's or Tech MBA. Before Stage 2 drafts Standard 9 for those two programmes, either a similar review/feedback exercise should be run for them, or Stage 2 needs to honestly state the review mechanism is designed but not yet exercised — Didem should decide which before drafting starts, since it affects how strong a claim can honestly be made.

6. **Standard 8 (Public Information) — no evidence of an actual public website or published fee/employment information for any programme, and no public-facing document of any kind for Tech MBA.** Lower urgency than the above since brochures exist for two of three programmes and this is more a "does the website need to be built/audited" operational question than a drafting blocker, but Stage 2 will not be able to cite real published content without someone confirming what is actually live on the institution's site today.
