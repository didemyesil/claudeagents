# Curriculum Orientation, Professional Master's in Applied AI

The programme is built on two complementary focus areas — referred to as learning lines — that are embedded in both content and assessment. Together, they ensure that graduates become job-ready AI professionals with reflective practitioner-research skills.

## Learning Line 1: Applied Artificial Intelligence

Our Applied AI spine equips learners to navigate the complete AI solution life cycle: from framing business questions, exploring and modelling data, to deploying models in the cloud and monitoring them in production. This is aligned with industry-standard frameworks such as EDISON. Technical modules are linked to authentic projects informed by current industry needs, so learners graduate with a portfolio of production-level artefacts that demonstrate job-ready skills. Continuous dialogue with industry representatives and faculty ensures that new capabilities, such as ML-Ops or responsible AI practices, are integrated into the curriculum for relevance.

## Learning Line 2: Applied Research

This learning line is built around an action research methodology, which integrates inquiry, reflection, and change into professional practice. Learners design and conduct research projects that address real organisational challenges, producing knowledge while delivering value. The line culminates in the Final Project, where a professional problem is tackled through an AI solution studied with an action-research lens.

Leadership development is embedded into this methodology through the framework of First-, Second-, and Third-Person Inquiry, supporting growth at multiple levels and directly strengthening learners' capacity as AI professionals:

- **First-Person Inquiry (Self-Leadership):** Learners critically reflect on their personal values, assumptions, and biases. This enhances their ability to question problem framings, identify ethical implications, and ensure AI projects are rigorous and responsible.
- **Second-Person Inquiry (Interpersonal Leadership):** Learners develop relational awareness skills to collaborate effectively. This supports their role as AI professionals in interdisciplinary teams, eliciting insights, integrating perspectives, and co-creating solutions.
- **Third-Person Inquiry (Organisational Leadership):** Learners analyse systemic dynamics and design responsible interventions that influence organisations. This strengthens their capacity to frame research questions at the organisational level, align AI solutions with strategic priorities, and assess broader societal impact.

In sum, graduates leave the programme with:

- a portfolio of AI solutions demonstrating advanced technical capacity, and
- a final project that combines technical expertise with action research and inquiry-based leadership, showcasing their ability to generate knowledge while delivering value at the professional master's level.
