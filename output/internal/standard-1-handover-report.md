   directly whether the QA policy is public. The honest ex-ante answer
   (internal-only now, public once operational) is defensible for an

   differ (documented explicitly in the adapted copies), which is a
   reasonable answer, but panel scrutiny should be expected.

   open; if unresolved by submission, Standard 1's self-assessed position
   should not be upgraded past "Satisfactory."
