# **PROGRAMME** **OBJECTIVES & OUTCOMES**

The Tech MBA programme aims to:

  

  - equip you with the knowledge and skills to strategically **integrate AI technologies** into business strategies

  

  - develop agile leaders who can harness the power of emerging technologies to **adapt to and lead change in organisations**

  

  - **foster an entrepreneurial mindset** by exploring sustainable business models and growth strategies to **innovate** in organisations

  
  

In line with these objectives, the **programme outcomes** are structured as follows: 

  

*(The outcomes of the Tech MBA programme align with* ***European Qualification Framework (EQF)*** *Cycle 2 masters level descriptors and* ***the Netherlands Qualification Framework (NLQF)*** *for professional masters)*

  

**Professional Development and Ethical Conduct**

  

You will exhibit a high level of professional development and learning ability, with a specific focus on strategically integrating AI technologies into business strategies. Ethical and moral conduct will be inherent in your work, ensuring that your contributions align with the highest ethical standards while harnessing the power of emerging technologies for organisational change.

  

**Problem-Solving and Action Research**

  

You will demonstrate the ability to independently recognize, analyse, and solve complex problems in technology, leadership, and innovation, particularly in the context of agile leadership and technology integration. You will apply participative research methods and leadership skills effectively while considering ethical, moral, and legal considerations, emphasising their application in driving organisational change.

  

**Multidisciplinary Collaboration**

  

You will effectively collaborate with professionals from diverse disciplines and perspectives, emphasising the integration of AI technologies into business strategies and sustainable growth models. You will communicate your technological interpretations, practical research setup, implications, and results professionally, both in written/visual and spoken form, with a focus on fostering an entrepreneurial mindset and driving innovation.

  

**Innovative Impact**

  

You will innovate existing knowledge and practices at the intersection of technology, leadership aligning these innovations with strategic AI integration, agile leadership, and sustainable business models. Your contributions will result in a tangible impact on the professional practice and the broader professional domain, emphasising the innovative use of AI and technology for growth and change in organisations.

  

# **THE LEARNING** **EXPERIENCE**

  

The Tech MBA programme is a fully online degree programme designed to be completed in approximately **20 months**, offering **60 ECTS** credits towards a professional master's degree.

  

The Tech MBA programme offers a dynamic and interactive online learning experience that combines workshops, case labs, assignments, mentoring, action learning sets, access to a digital library, and insights from global guest speakers. This multifaceted approach ensures that you not only acquire knowledge but also gain practical skills, collaborative experience, and a global perspective in the domains of technology, leadership, innovation, and growth.

  

**Workshops**

  

Throughout the programme, you will participate in interactive weekly workshops and engaging cases. These sessions are facilitated by experienced Course Facilitators who guide you through practical applications of technology, leadership, and innovation concepts. Workshops provide a dynamic environment for collaborative learning and problem-solving.

  

**Assignments & Projects**

  

Your learning journey includes a mix of assignments and module projects, all completed online. These assignments and projects are carefully designed to challenge you in applying your knowledge to real-world scenarios. They encourage critical thinking and creativity, reflecting the programme's focus on fostering an entrepreneurial mindset and innovative problem-solving.

  

**Digital Library**

  

Access to a vast digital library is a cornerstone of your learning experience. This resource-rich library provides you with comprehensive materials enabling you to explore the module content in depth. The digital library supports your self-directed learning.

  

**Group Mentoring**

  

The programme places a strong emphasis on group mentoring to ensure your success. You will have the opportunity to connect with an experienced mentor who provides guidance and insights tailored to your career aspirations and learning objectives. The mentoring component enriches your understanding of practical applications and industry insights.

  

**Action Learning Sets**

  

Facilitated by your action learning coach, action learning sets are a key feature of the programme, promoting peer-to-peer learning and collaboration. You will work closely with your peers, engaging in structured discussions and problem-solving activities. These sets foster multidisciplinary collaboration and encourage you to explore the intersection of technology, leadership, and innovation.

  

**Global Guest Speakers**

  

The programme enhances your learning experience with insights from global guest speakers who are experts in technology, leadership, and innovation. The guest speakers share their real-world experiences and perspectives, providing valuable industry insights and broadening your understanding of global trends and best practices.

  

Flexibility and adaptability are integral to the programme's design. While the programme provides structured learning opportunities, you are encouraged to take ownership of your learning journey. You can tailor your schedule to accommodate your professional commitments while participating in learning activities regularly. 

  

On average, dedicating approximately **20 hours per week** to your studies is recommended for success, with additional time allocated for module projects, which may require more in-depth engagement.

# **THE PROGRAMME** **COMPETENCIES & LEARNING OUTCOMES**

  

**Digital transformation and strategies** 

  

  - Demonstrate a deep understanding of the drivers behind digital transformation to formulate well-structured strategies 

  

  - Effectively assess the role of technology as a disruptor and enabler of change within various business contexts 

  

  - Develop a sound competitive positioning and a future-proof business strategy that aligns with your organisational vision and objectives

  

**Data leadership and strategic utilisation of data**

  

  - Assess the significance of data leadership in contemporary organisations.

  

  - Analyse the key principles of data management and governance for effective utilisation.

  

  - Formulate data-driven strategies that drive business growth and innovation.

  

  - Communicate the importance of data literacy and cultivate a data-driven culture within organisations effectively.

  
  

**Technology integration into business** 

  

  - Assess the strategic value of the selected technology within a business context.

  

  - Analyse the potential impact of technology integration on organisational processes and outcomes.

  

  - Formulate strategies for the effective and sustainable integration of the chosen technology into diverse business domains.

  
  
  

**AI strategy building**

  

  - Evaluate the fundamental principles of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Generative AI.

  

  - Analyse the diverse applications of AI, including Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI, in various business contexts.

  

  - Formulate AI business strategies that leverage machine, deep learning, and generative AI capabilities to address specific business challenges.

  

  - Communicate the value, potential, and transformative impact of AI strategies within organisations effectively.

  
  

**Ethical and responsible AI integration into business**

  

  - Evaluate ethical considerations associated with AI implementation in business operations, ensuring alignment with responsible practices and societal values.

  

  - Analyse the potential social and environmental consequences of AI technologies within a business context, emphasising sustainability, corporate responsibility, and positive societal contributions.

  

  - Interpret AI governance and regulatory frameworks, ensuring that AI-driven business practices comply with legal and ethical standards to minimise risks and promote responsible AI use.

  

  - Anticipate future ethical challenges by exploring the concept of singularity and its potential impact on AI integration into business, preparing to address evolving ethical dilemmas.

  

**Change Management** 

  

  - Demonstrate understanding of change management principles, strategies, and tools to analyse the impact of change and develop comprehensive change management plans.

  

  - Identify and address sources of resistance to change within organisations, leveraging effective strategies and communication.

  

  - Evaluate and select appropriate change management frameworks and apply them to guide organisational change efforts.

  

  - Develop strategies to enhance organisational resilience in dynamic environments.

  

**Agile technology leadership**

  - Demonstrate a deep understanding of modern approaches to effective leadership in the technology domain, with a specific focus on Servant Leadership principles.

  

  - Apply collaborative, adaptable, and results-driven leadership strategies within a technology context as an Agile Leader.

  

  - Utilise Agile leadership principles to enhance collaboration, adaptability, and team performance within technology projects or initiatives, emphasising Agile teams.

  

  - Integrate Agile leadership practices across different organisational levels, demonstrating adaptability and promoting a culture of continuous improvement within technology teams and Agile organisations.

  
  

**Managing business through OKRs**

  

  - Effectively define and explain the concept of Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) and their significance in organisational goal alignment.

  

  - Demonstrate a high level understanding in implementing OKRs within an organisation, including setting, tracking, and evaluating objectives and key results.

  

  - Analyse the importance of continuous improvement through CFRs (Conversations, Feedback, and Recognition) in the context of OKRs.

  

  - Illustrate the iterative nature of the OKR life cycle and its impact on goal achievement within organisations.

  

**Agile product management** 

  

  - Apply Agile principles and an Agile mindset to the product development process.

  

  - Design an Agile product roadmap that aligns with organisational goals and adapts to changing market conditions.

  

  - Implement Agile methodologies to build and validate products iteratively, incorporating user feedback effectively.

  

  - Execute product sprints using Agile techniques, emphasising continuous improvement in product development processes.

  
  

**Design thinking**

  

  -  Successfully collaborate within interdisciplinary teams, integrating diverse perspectives and using visual canvases to co-create and communicate innovative solutions that drive creativity and address complex problems.

  

  - Apply human-centred design principles to identify and empathise with user needs, facilitating the creation of innovative solutions that address real-world problems.

  
  

**Innovative and sustainable business development**

  

  - Formulate innovative business models that balance sustainability and growth within the dynamic tech industry landscape.

  

  - Apply the Business Model Canvas to design, iterate, and optimise sustainable business models, aligning them with the fast-paced tech sector.

  

  - Devise and implement comprehensive sustainability strategies that meet both social and environmental commitments, ensuring sustainable tech business development.

  

  - Evaluate and adapt innovative sustainability initiatives, fostering lasting success and adaptability in tech-related ventures.

  
  
  
  
  

**Growth hacking**

  

  - Display understanding in growth hacking strategies to explore new channels and platforms, enhancing customer acquisition, and optimising conversion rates to stimulate business growth and innovation.

  

  - Analyse data to make informed decisions in the growth hacking process, utilising insights to improve business practices and adapt to changing market conditions effectively.

  

  - Develop customer acquisition plans to efficiently target and reach potential clients, resulting in increased customer base expansion.

  

  - Evaluate automation techniques in growth hacking efforts, streamlining processes and ensuring the scalability of innovative practices for sustainable business development. 

  
  

**Action research** 

  

  - Evaluate technological  contexts, collect and analyse in-depth information and present the new information in a scientific way.

**Coaching**

  

  - Practice and use a coaching model and steps as an inquiry and leadership tool as part of your action research practices in a technology context.

  

**Team Facilitation**

  

  - Practice and use facilitation techniques as an inquiry and leadership tool as part of your action research practices in a technology context.

  

**Communication**

  

  - Propose ideas in a clear and concise manner orally, visually and in writing to initiate change or influence your context.

  

  - Appropriately adapt your message, style, and tone to accommodate a variety of audiences such as peers, specialists, non-specialists, superiors or clients in a professional context.

  

  - Respectfully listen to group members to gain a full understanding of issues.

**Interpersonal awareness**

**Team-play**

**Being a trusted advisor**

**Self-learning**

  - Recognize your own learning style and adapt your self-learning process accordingly.

# **ASSESSMENT** **OF YOUR LEARNING** 

Our assessment strategy is designed to evaluate your progress, learning outcomes, and overall performance throughout the Tech MBA programme. This multifaceted approach ensures that you have ample opportunities to demonstrate your understanding, skills, and application of the programme content.

  

**Module Projects (1 per module) -** **50% Weight**

  

 Module projects are central to your learning experience. They represent a substantial portion of your assessment, allowing you to dive deep into each module's content. These projects are evaluated by faculty members, providing you with expert feedback on your work.

  

**Weekly Tasks/Activities (1 per week) - 30% Weight** 

We believe in the power of continuous learning. Weekly tasks and activities can take various forms, including discussion activities on our Canvas platform. These activities foster peer-learning and can also be peer-reviewed. They constitute a significant part of your assessment, contributing to your overall learning experience and development.

  
  

**Action Learning Reflective Accounts (1 per module) - 10% Weight** 

Reflective accounts provide you with a space for introspection and personal growth. These accounts are intended to help you reflect on the module's content and apply the principles of action learning. Action learning is a methodology that encourages you to take your learning into real contexts, bringing those experiences back to the group for reflection and growth.

  

**Knowledge Checks (1 for each week) - 10% Weight** 

To reinforce your understanding and retention of the weekly material, we have included knowledge checks. These assessments, administered weekly, make up a portion of your overall assessment and serve as opportunities for ongoing learning and self-assessment.

  

**Digital Badges and Certificates** 

  

You will earn two types of badges to award your effort and performance during the programme.

  

  - **Community learning badge:** This will be awarded when you actively participate in the discussions on Canvas.

  

  - **Module completion badges**: This badge will be awarded when you successfully complete all required work within a module. 

  

  - **Certificates:** This will be issued when you successfully complete a certification with a passing score of at least 60. 

  

You can showcase your digital badges and certificates on your social media such as LinkedIn.

  

**Passing Requirement in the Certificates:**

  

To successfully pass each certificate, you must accumulate **a minimum of 60 points** across all assessments.

# **FINAL PROJECT**

  

### **Objectives in the Final Project**

The final project is the last part of your programme and the successful completion of your professional master. It is a chance for you to put all the competencies you learned - both technology and leadership - into practice. The outcome is intended to be a submission of a work-based project along with **a written report** and **a presentation**. The principal question to ask is **“How can I improve my practice?”** With this question in mind, this part of the programme offers a practical and structured way of applying action research in a workplace, organisation or independently.

The project you undertake may be driven by an organisational need or aim for a social good. It may therefore be designed in relation to several needs:

\- to solve a work-based problem within an organisation;

\- to review an existing aspect of an organisational practice;

\- to launch a digital transformation strategy

\- to enquire into a particular area with a view to developing it;

\- to introduce a new methodology for doing something in a different way

\- to introduce a solution to a social problem 

  

### **Assessment of your Final Project**

  

  - 15% Project Plan
  - 30% Action Research Methodology 
  - 40% Strategic Technology Focus
  - 15% Presentation

Please see **Appendix 4** and **5** for project report format, **Appendix 6** for the assessment process of your final project and **Appendix 7** for the evaluation criteria of your final project report.

Your action research practice begins with your leadership modules. This phase of the programme integrates action research methodology with your leadership practice. By the start of the Final Project phase, you will have constructed your project idea, planned your actions, presented your project plan and got it approved by the organisation and your supervisor.  

  

### **Methodology** 

In order to progress towards a successful outcome of your project, we employ a method called **Action Research**. as a research methodology which develops knowledge through being and doing. In other words, it Is grounded in your own lived experience aiming to develop a living theory of practice development. You will do that by asking a fundamental but profound  question: “How can I improve my practice?”  As an action researcher, you will be able to  create an educational influence in your own learning, in the learning of others and in the learning of the social formation in which you live and work.

*“Action research is a situated activity that locates the observer in the world.  \[It\] consists of a set of interpretive, material practices that make the world visible.  These practices transform the world.  They turn the world into a series of representations, including field notes, interviews, conversations, photographs, recordings, and memos to the self. This means that qualitative researchers study things in their natural settings, attempting to make sense of or interpret phenomena in terms of the meanings people bring to them.”*

**Action research: How does it work?** 

**  
**This research methodology proposes an iterative research cycle as below. 

*Constructing* What is my inquiry, and why does it matter?

*Planning action* With whom, with what, and how will I inquire?

*Taking action* What is the evidence of my data?

*Evaluating action* To what extent are the outcomes in line with my intention, my plan and my data? What more needs to happen?

Your project will be conducted in two cycles. Once you complete your project activities in cycle 1, you will review your previous actions, reflect on them and improve them in cycle 2. 

  
  
  
  
  
  
  

### **Action Research Basic Recipe**

1.  Articulate the action research question :   How can I…?    …in relation to these issues that present for me and others in this situation, within this context and culture, for whom it matters because…
2.  Develop a stakeholder network map  :   …of those who affect or are affected by your action research. Who needs to be involved?  Who has influence in the system?
3.  Design a participative process  :   …that is clear about the degree of participation appropriate at each stage from articulating the research question to evaluating the results
4.  Consider what evidence will be needed  :   …as quantitative data and qualitative experiences to provide indicators of improvement and achievement
5.  Plan to analyse data and incorporate stakeholder perspectives as findings  :   …in discussions on outcomes in relation to the intended achievement and its benefits
6.  Develop an action plan  :   …with those who are able to make the research happen
7.  Reflect on learning  :   ..to evaluate the personal, collective and systemic development experiences from participating in the action research
