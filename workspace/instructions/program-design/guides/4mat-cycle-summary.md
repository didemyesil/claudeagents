# 4MAT summary (extracted by the coordinator; the source is the PDF in Drive)

## 4MAT Cycle.pdf (Dalene Mactier 2013; Drive 1olo_l8U1xEsVVxPJaZYYIV2JbJhAWNgI)
- Cycle: 4 quadrants, 8 steps. Q1 WHY: 1 Connect [right mode], 2 Attend [left]. Q2 WHAT: 3 Image [right], 4 Inform [left]. Q3 HOW: 5 Practice [left], 6 Extend [right]. Q4 WHAT IF: 7 Refine [left], 8 Perform [right].
- 4 learner/facilitator types: Type 1 Imaginative ("Why?"), Type 2 Analytical ("What?"), Type 3 Common sense ("How does this work?"), Type 4 Dynamic ("What if?"). There is no hierarchy.
- Teaching intentions and strategy examples per quadrant: Q1 icebreakers, sharing stories, appreciative inquiry, journals, partner work, role play. Q2 expert knowledge, looking for patterns, note taking, structured tasks, classification. Q3 hands-on practice, mastery, games, reflective writing, making resources. Q4 adapting, synthesis, future use, portfolios, "light bulb moments", a question for the next session.
- Brooks-Harris & Stock-Ward (1999) group activities: Engaging (icebreaker, motivation grabber, stimulus role-play, gallery, guided fantasy), Informing (lecturette, group survey, instrument, demonstration, modelling role-play, case, movement/sorting, handout), Involving (practice role-play, simulation, worksheet, structured discussion, art work, scenario, skit), Planning (personal practice, action plan, goal setting, brainstorming solutions, homework).
- Example icebreaker list (Truths & Lies, bingo, burning questions, interviewing, good or new, continuum, getting active).
- NOTE: the source comes from a Playcentre (early childhood / parent) context; it needs adapting to higher education and the Amsterdam Tech context.
