## 1. Purpose

Team Academy is committed to ensuring that gender, and other personal
characteristics unrelated to academic or professional merit, do not create
barriers to admission, learning, assessment, or participation in the
institution's governance and quality-assurance activities. This statement
brings together, in one place, the equal-opportunities and
non-discrimination commitments that are currently set out separately in the
institution's admissions, assessment, examination, and external-stakeholder
governance documents.

## 2. Admissions

All applicants "from diverse backgrounds, cultures, genders and
nationalities, regardless of their physical disability, are encouraged to
apply to Team Academy programmes." Admissions decisions are made by the
Admissions Consultant / Admissions Team against the same published entry
requirements for every applicant, regardless of gender or other personal
characteristics not related to academic or professional readiness.

## 3. Learning environment and assessment

Team Academy's programmes aim to provide "an inclusive and accessible
learning environment that ensures all learners, regardless of personal
circumstances, are given equal opportunities to demonstrate their
knowledge, understanding, and skills." The institution's Policy on
Assessment commits to fostering "a diverse and inclusive learning
environment, recognising and respecting intercultural differences that may
influence assessment dynamics," and requires assessors to "design and
evaluate assessments that are free from cultural assumptions or references
unfamiliar to some learners." Reasonable adjustments and accommodations are
provided to learners with disabilities, chronic illness, neurodivergence, or
other special personal circumstances "in line with individual needs,"
handled confidentially, "promoting equal opportunities for success."

## 4. Governance and external-stakeholder bodies

Team Academy takes gender and background diversity into account when
composing the bodies that oversee and advise on quality and assessment:

- Members of the Examination Board "preferably are evenly distributed in
  age, gender and cultural background."
- When selecting new Field Representatives, the institution takes into
  consideration "status, gender, race, ethnicity, age and geographic
  locations," and the group is explicitly tasked with assisting "the
  university in the identification of new board members for wider
  diversity."

## 5. What this statement does not yet claim

This is a consolidation of existing provisions, not evidence of a separate,
long-standing, freestanding gender-equality policy, nor of any measured
outcomes (e.g. gender balance data on staff, faculty, or learner cohorts) —
no such data exists yet for programmes that have not launched. Team Academy
does not yet operate a dedicated equality/diversity office, complaints
channel specific to discrimination, or published equality monitoring data;
if the institution wishes to state an intention to develop any of these,
that must come from Didem as a genuine institutional decision, not be
assumed here.
