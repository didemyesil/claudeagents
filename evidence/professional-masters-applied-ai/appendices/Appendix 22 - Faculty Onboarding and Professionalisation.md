# Faculty Onboarding & Professionalisation

### Step 1: Job Advertisement and Application

- Post job advertisements on relevant platforms.
- Clearly outline the required qualifications and competencies: a minimum of a Master's degree, preferably a PhD, in a relevant field related to AI/Data Science and Leadership, and industry experience in the related field. Preferably teaching/training and assessment experience. Effective interpersonal skills and commitment to continuous learning.

### Step 2: Selection Process

- Screen applications based on qualifications and experience.
- Conduct initial interviews for cultural fit and to get an understanding of the candidate's teaching & assessment experience.
- If necessary, conduct a practical assessment on the candidate's teaching & assessment competency level (e.g. requesting a workshop plan on a selected topic).

### Step 3: Initial Hiring and Pre-boarding

- Send offer letters and complete necessary hiring paperwork.
- Provide official university information & guidelines (e.g. Official Handbooks and faculty expectations).

### Step 4: Onboarding and Orientation

- Set up University Accounts.
- Conduct an orientation session covering: university policies and procedures; introduction to the academic programme and curriculum; introduction to the platforms & tools used in the programmes; overview of expectations.
- Engage in a criterion-based reflective conversation to create awareness of the development options/necessities.

### Step 5: Initial Contribution to Professional Development

- Provide information & guidance on the **educator competencies** (curriculum design, facilitative teaching, assessment), depending on the role and the competency level of the faculty member.

### Step 6: Provide (more structured) Continuous Professional Development through Action Inquiry Methodology

- Continue to offer our faculty members opportunities for professional development using the action inquiry methodology on the required educator competencies.
