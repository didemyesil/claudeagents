# IAAR (Specialised) Checklist — Cluster: Applied AI / Applied Computing / Tech MBA

Source: `/accreditors/iaar-specialised.md` (transcribed verbatim from *IAAR Standards
and Guidelines for International Accreditation of Foreign Educational
Organisations and Educational Programmes (based on ESG)*, IAAR, 2018, §III pp.
12–22; local copy: `/accreditors/sources/iaar-international-specialised-standards-2018.pdf`)
Status: fully sourced

> **Scope note (revised):** The 10 standards, their required evidence types,
> and panel lens tags below are defined at **accreditor level** — they don't
> vary by programme, so this single checklist run covers the entire
> **3-programme cluster** named in the config's "Active application context"
> (Professional Master's in Applied AI; Bachelor's in Applied Computing;
> Tech MBA), matching how IAAR itself structures a cluster accreditation: one
> Self-Assessment Report, submitted once, covering all EPs in the cluster
> (config's own rule: "aspects common to all programmes are described once...
> to avoid repetition"). Stage 2 (`folder-developer`) is what determines,
> standard by standard and even sentence by sentence, which content is
> genuinely shared across the 3 programmes (written once) versus
> programme-specific (written separately per programme) — that judgment
> depends on the actual evidence, not on this checklist, so it is not forced
> here.

> **Ex-ante note (carried forward from config):** This is an **initial
> accreditation (ex-ante)** assessment. Per the config's own format rule, the
> "achievement data for the last 3–5 years" requirement is explicitly **waived**
> for this application. Wherever a standard's evidence type below could be read
> as requiring a demonstrated track record (e.g. mobility history, review
> cycles, prior monitoring data, prior external QA results), Stage 2
> (`folder-developer`) must reframe it as **design/plan/intention** evidence —
> not invent achieved-outcomes data to fill the gap. This is flagged per
> standard below where relevant.

## Standard 1 — Policy for Quality Assurance
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Published QA policy document; stakeholder involvement
  records; evidence of QA policy revision history and satisfaction assessment
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Industry Representative; Student
- Note: Config's Guidelines text explicitly calls for involvement of external
  stakeholders and students in policy development — hence multi-lens tag. For
  ex-ante purposes, "revision history and satisfaction assessment" should be
  presented as planned review mechanism, not historical data (no prior cycle
  exists yet for this new programme).

## Standard 2 — Design and Approval of Programmes
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Documented EP design/approval procedure; intended
  learning outcomes mapped to NQF/QF-EHEA level; curriculum structure, ECTS
  workload, module descriptions; external EP examination records
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Subject Expert; Industry
  Representative
- Note: Sample questions reference "professional-environment requirements"
  and external examination of the EP — justifies Industry Representative and
  Subject Expert tags alongside Education & Assessment Expert. "External EP
  examination records" is ex-ante-sensitive: for a new programme this is
  likely a planned/committed external review step rather than a completed one
  — must not be presented as already executed unless it genuinely has been.

## Standard 3 — Student-Centred Learning, Teaching and Assessment
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Description of pedagogical methods and delivery
  modes used; feedback system records; published assessment criteria/marking
  criteria; student complaints/appeal procedure
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Student
- Note: None.

## Standard 4 — Student Admission, Progression, Recognition and Certification
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Published admission/progression/recognition/
  certification regulations; Lisbon Convention-aligned recognition mechanism;
  ENIC/NARIC cooperation evidence; graduation documentation templates
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Student
- Note: "Progression" evidence should be framed as the designed
  monitoring/regulatory mechanism for this ex-ante application, not
  demonstrated student outcomes (no cohort yet exists).

## Standard 5 — Teaching Staff
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Recruitment/employment policy documents; staff
  qualifications vs. EQF/industry framework; professional development
  records; practitioner-involvement records; academic mobility records
  (5-year)
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Subject Expert; Industry
  Representative
- Note: Config's sample questions explicitly ask whether "industry
  practitioners [are] involved in teaching" — Industry Representative lens
  justified. **"Academic mobility records (5-year)" is a 3–5 year track-record
  evidence type per the config's own table; per the config's ex-ante note this
  requirement is relaxed for this application** — Stage 2 should present staff
  qualifications/experience to date rather than force a 5-year mobility
  history for a not-yet-running programme.

## Standard 6 — Learning Resources and Student Support
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Funding/infrastructure inventory vs. EP goals;
  library/IT/plagiarism-check/Wi-Fi resource description; student support
  procedure documentation; safety compliance evidence
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Student
- Note: None.

## Standard 7 — Information Management
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Description of information collection/analysis
  system; KPI and student-progression data; stakeholder involvement in
  information use; risk identification records
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert
- Note: Config's Guidelines/sample questions for this standard are framed
  institutionally (information systems, internal QA feedback loops) without a
  clear subject-matter, industry, or student-specific angle — single-lens tag
  reflects that, not an omission. "KPI and student-progression data" is
  ex-ante-sensitive: for a new programme this must be the designed
  data-collection system, not actual collected data (none exists pre-launch).

## Standard 8 — Public Information
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Published information across EO web resources
  covering programmes, outcomes, qualifications, procedures, fees,
  employment; satisfaction-with-information records
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Student; Industry Representative
- Note: Config's sample questions list "employment opportunities" and
  "partner cooperation" among published-information items — justifies
  Industry Representative alongside Student (prospective-student-facing
  information) and Education & Assessment Expert.

## Standard 9 — On-going Monitoring and Periodic Review of Programmes
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Documented EP review procedure and records;
  stakeholder (student/employer) involvement in review; published revised
  programme specifications
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert; Subject Expert; Industry
  Representative; Student
- Note: Config's Guidelines explicitly name "changing societal needs,"
  "latest discipline research," and involvement of "students and other
  stakeholders" — all four personas justified. **This standard is
  inherently track-record-oriented (monitoring/review cycles); per the
  config's ex-ante note, for this application it must be presented as the
  designed review mechanism/plan, not as evidence of reviews already
  conducted** — no prior cycle can exist for a programme not yet delivered.

## Standard 10 — Cyclical External Quality Assurance
- Source status: verified
- Required evidence type: Record of prior external QA participation and
  results (if any — first-time/initial accreditation may have none);
  evidence of how prior QA findings were acted upon
- Panel lens: Education & Assessment Expert
- Note: Config's Guidelines/sample questions frame this standard
  institutionally (prior external QA cycles, national/international
  evaluations) with no clear subject-matter, industry, or student-specific
  angle — single-lens tag reflects that. Config itself flags this evidence
  type as possibly absent for initial accreditation ("if any — first-time/
  initial accreditation may have none") — this is carried forward verbatim,
  not resolved by me. Stage 2 must not invent a prior-QA record where none
  exists; state absence plainly if that is the case for this applicant.
