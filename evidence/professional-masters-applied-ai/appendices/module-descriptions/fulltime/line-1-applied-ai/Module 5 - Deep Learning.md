# Module 5: Deep Learning (Full Time)

**Code:** 05.AI | **Learning Line:** Applied Artificial Intelligence | **ECs:** 4

This module develops foundational and applied capability in deep learning: the mathematical/conceptual basis of neural networks (weights, layers, loss functions, optimisation, activation functions); implementation with Keras (structured problems) and PyTorch (configurable architectures, performance tuning); designing networks for business-relevant use cases; comparing deep learning with conventional ML by data type, complexity, interpretability, and computational demands. Concludes with image-processing techniques in Python and deep-learning applications in computer vision.

## Connection with Applied Research

Designing and applying deep learning models requires choices about data, methods, and context; carrying an inquiry mindset means questioning assumptions, justifying design decisions, and reflecting on their impact in professional settings.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Deep Learning** — Analyse the mathematical foundations and underlying logic of neural networks; develop neural networks to address complex problems; design advanced neural network architectures to solve business problems; justify the selection of deep learning architectures vs traditional ML models.

**Computer Vision** — Justify the need for advanced CV methods depending on the data; develop deep learning architectures to address complex CV problems.
