# Module 4: Interpersonal Leadership Inquiry (Full Time)

**Code:** 04.AR | **Learning Line:** Applied Research | **ECs:** 6

Introduces tools for collecting and synthesising diverse forms of knowledge (interviews, observations, reflective journals, collaborative workshops; combining qualitative and quantitative data). Expands into second-person inquiry: leadership as a relational practice — appreciative mindset, deep listening, powerful questions and feedback. Practises peer/team coaching through the CLEAR model as a structured conversational approach.

## Connection with Applied AI

Generating meaningful qualitative evidence strengthens integration of contextual/practice-based knowledge into technical inquiries; second-person inquiry skills (appreciative mindset, deep listening, powerful questions, feedback) enhance collaboration in data-driven environments; the CLEAR model supports communicating insights and engaging stakeholders.

## Target Competencies and Learning Outcomes

**Interpersonal Leadership** — Engage in one-to-one or group conversations using appropriate communication tools to achieve results; critically reflect on interpersonal experience as a facilitator in group settings.

**Research Methodology for Professional Practice** — Apply action research methodology with qualitative/participatory inquiry approaches to design interventions in professional contexts.

## Learning Resources
