# Organisational Structure — Professional Master's in Applied AI

## Role Descriptions

**The Executive Board** provides overall governance and strategic direction for Team Academy.

**The Examination Board** is responsible for ensuring the quality and integrity of assessment processes, overseeing examination standards, and safeguarding the academic quality of the programmes and the level of the diploma.

**QA Team** provides quality assurance oversight, ensuring academic standards and quality measures are maintained.

**The Educational Council (Student Advisory Board)** consists of student representatives who share their experience of the programme quality and provide advisory input. Their recommendations are passed to the Managing Director, who shares them with the Programme Managers and Programme Design Leads.

**Managing Director** Oversees operational management of Team Academy, coordinating between different departments and implementing strategic decisions from the Executive Board.

**Programme Manager(s)** manage programme administration and coordination between faculty, students and the programme design lead.

**Programme Design & Development Lead** Responsible for designing and developing educational programmes, ensuring curriculum quality and improvement.

**Faculty** Academic staff who design and deliver learning activities such as workshops, design and develop assessments as part of the curriculum team, as well as evaluate the assessments.

**Head Coach** Supervises and coordinates the team coaching function, working directly with Team Coaches.

**Team Coaches** provide coaching support to student teams' learning.

**Admissions Manager** Handles student admissions processes.

**Finance** manages financial operations, budgeting, and financial reporting.

**Marketing** Specialist for marketing activities and external communications.

**Web Development** Specialist for maintaining the web presence of Team Academy.
