# Document Scope Register

The decision layer for the application. A document's scope is settled here
before it is produced, adapted or cited. Every later stage obeys this file.

It exists because "shared with the Master's" was once implemented as "produce a
separate Tech MBA copy" — the opposite of the decision. Scope is recorded as a
value, not inferred from prose.

## Scope values

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `INSTITUTION` | One document for the whole institution. No per-programme copies. Every programme cites the same document. |
| `MASTERS-LEVEL` | One document covering the Professional Master's in Applied AI and the Tech MBA. Common provisions once; provisions for one only in a programme-specific annex. |
| `BACHELORS` | The Bachelor's in Applied Computing has its own document, reflecting NLQF level 6 entry, progression and graduation rules. |
| `PROGRAMME` | Genuinely one per programme — the content differs by its nature. |

## Reference — what IAAR and Dutch law require

IAAR does not prescribe how an institution structures its internal regulatory
documents. Its Standard 1 asks how far the programmes "comply with the
regulatory documents of the EO", which assumes documents at institution level
and tests programme conformity against them. Its cluster rule — "aspects common
to all programmes are described once" — governs the report, not the document
set.

The binding constraint is Dutch. Team Academy is a recognised legal entity for
higher education under the WHW, which requires regulations "for each programme
or group of programmes", so grouping is expressly permitted. Standard practice
in the Netherlands groups by level: one set for the bachelor's programmes, one
for the master's, each with a common part and a programme-specific annex.
Bachelor-only provisions — the propedeuse, the binding study advice, entry on a
secondary-school diploma — have no master's equivalent.

Aligning with the national pattern is itself an answer to IAAR, whose Standard 1
requires the policy to take account of the national context.

## Rule

A document is produced only after its scope is settled here. Where a scope says
one document covers several programmes, that means **one file** — a copy bearing
another programme's name is a breach of this register, not an implementation of
it.
